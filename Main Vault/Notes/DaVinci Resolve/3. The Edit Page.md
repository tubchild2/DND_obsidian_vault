
**Function**
- The hub for everything that happens in your video

**Media Pool**
- Functions identically to the media pool panel from the media page.\

**Source Viewer**
- On the left
- Lets you preview a piece of media from the media pool
- Akin to picking something up and looking at it, without adding it to your plate
- Source Tape Mode takes all of your footage and loads it into the source viewer and lets you view it all as once contiguous tape.
- You can set an IN and OUT, where the beginning and the end of the clip are with I and O.
- J reverses, L pauses I think, L plays at normal speed I think

**Timeline Viewer**
- On the right

**Timeline**
- Lets you add clips to your video
- You can move clips to the left or right, represents back and forth in time on your video
- You can zoom in and out
- You can expand or shrink video and audio tracks
- You can slice the beginning and ends by mouse overing the edges and dragging it
- Ctrl + F goes fullscreen
- You can split a clip with the razorblade tool, or by using CTRL + \
- You can select empty spaces and click delete to cut blank sections of the timeline
- The magnet tool causes clips to snap together at their edges
- You can link and unlink audio and video clips with the chain tool
- You can delete clips with the backspace key.
- Insertion
	- The insert clip tool shifts everything forward to insert a clip from the media pool
	- Overwrite inserts by force and cuts everything in the way
	- Replace inserts itself and deletes the clip that's in the way
	- Inserts on top adds the video to a new track
	- Append on end inserts the clip at the end of the video
	- The trim tool lets you do live overwrite that you can edit
	- Ripple trim cuts things and moves them so that there's no blank space. CTRL + Shift + \[
- You can customize your keyboard shortcuts
- You can add transitions by selecting the space between cuts and right clicking to see options
- You can fade audio DB up and down by holding alt and moving the volume 
- You can fade video and audio by selecting the small white handles at the edges

**Inspector**
- When you select a clip, you can adjust its details and properties from the inspector
- Zoom, position, rotation, pitch, yaw, flip
- Cropping, composite (opacity), speed, stabilization, lens correction, scaling, etc.
- You can alter properties over time to create animations with keyframes
