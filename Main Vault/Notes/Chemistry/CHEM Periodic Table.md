#### Structure
**Groups**
- Columns labelled 1, 2, 3... etc.
- Each group shares the same number of valence electrons
- Share similar number of valence electrons / charge

**Elements**
- Atomic Symbol (H, He, Li, etc.)
- Atomic Number. Represents number of protons
- Atomic Mass. Represents weight in unified atomic mass units, or Daltons. Represented with the symbol u, or Da.

#### Groups
**Alkali Metals (1)**
- +1 Charge

**Alkaline Earth Metals (2)**
- +2 Charge

**Transition Metals (3-12)**
- No Standard Charge
- Zinc +2
- Cadmium +2
- Silver is +1

**Lanthanides (upper f-block)**
- No Standard Charge

**Actinides (lower f-block)**
- No Standard Charge

**Boron Group (13)**
- +3 Charge

**Carbon Group (14)**
- Usually shares electrons

**Pnictogens (15)**
- -3 Charge

**Chalcogens (16)**
- -2 Charge

**Halogens (17)**
- -1 Charge

**Noble Gasses (18)**
- 0 Charge
