### The Anxiety of Kindness Study
I want to see how people would react to compliments and what they'd assume about the person giving the compliment.

1. Does race/gender/height/weight play a part in it?
2. How much would it impact people's mood?
3. What would people assume about the person giving the compliment?
4. What are the potential social ramifications of giving people's compliments?
5. Would people who receive compliments feel obligated to pass it on?
6. How often to people give and receive compliments?
7. Does being in public incentivize or disincentivize giving people compliments?
8. Is there a certain level of anxiety speaking to strangers?
9. Is there a certain level of anxiety complimenting the people you know?
10. Does the knowledge of being in a study change how people answer?
