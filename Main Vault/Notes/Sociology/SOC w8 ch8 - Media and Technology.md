### Technology Today
**Technology**
	The application of science to address the problems of daily life. 
	Pencils and computers are both technology. 
	All aspects of our lives are influenced by technology.

**Technological Stratification**
	Form 1: Differential class-based access to technology
	Form 2: Knowledge gap. Ongoing and increasing gap in information for those who have access to technology. 

**E-Readiness**
	The ability to sort through, interpret, and process knowledge. 

**Digital Divide**
	The uneven access to technology among different races, classes, and geographic areas. 
	Coined by Al Gore
	Internet use varies a great deal by race, class, and age. Not by gender interestingly. 
	8 in 10 white people report owning a computer, only 6 in 10 black and Hispanic people did. 
	Black and Hispanic people were more likely to have smartphone only internet access. 
	Mostly rural counties had far lower rates of internet access. 
	By 2020, it was obvious it still exists.

**Online Romantic Relationships**
	Attachment. The bond that people form with each other. 
	Constant communication significantly increases the level of attachment. 
	However, the quality of the relationship was weaker or less satisfying when it was dominated by text.

**Reliance**
	Close friends who are heavily reliant on mobile devices and associated messaging my have more issues regarding overdependence on the relationship. 
	Guilt and pressure to respond (called entrapment) in mobile-dependent relationships leads to overall dissatisfaction. 

**Online Privacy, Security, and Control**
	Most people feel they have no control over their data and are concerned about its use. 
	Older Americans are more concerned than younger ones. 
	Black and Hispanic people are more likely than white people to think the government is tracking them.

**Net Neutrality**

### Global Implications
**Thomas Friedman** 2005
	Identified several ways in which technology flattens the globe and contributes to our global economy. 
	His book 'The World is Flat' posits that core economic concepts were changed by personal computing and high-speed internet.
	Access has allowed core-nation corporations to recruit workers in call centers in China or India. This new world order will exist whether core-nation businesses are ready or not. 
	Many people disagree and point out how innovation, economic activity, and population still gather in geographically attractive areas, and continue to create economic peaks and valleys. 

**Media Globalization**
	The worldwide integration of media through the cross-cultural exchange of ideas
	Lyons (2005) suggests multinational corporations are the primary vehicle of media globalization. 

**Media Consolidation**
	A process in which fewer and fewer owners control the majority of media outlets (Disney). 
	Consolidated media owes more to its stockholders than to the public. They control most of the media, and are controlled by the power elite, so they represent their political and social interests.

**Monopolies**

**Technological Globalization**

**Technological Diffusion**

**Functionalist**
	**Commercial Function**. 
		The 250 billion hours of TV watched annually in the US mean companies can connect with consumers like no else. Highly functional way to meet a market demographic where it lives. 
	**Entertainment Function**. 
		People simply enjoy it. 
	**Social Norm Functions**. 
		It helps us pass along norms, values, and beliefs to the next generation. Media teaches us what is good and bad, how we should speak, how we should behave, and how we should react to events. It also provides us with cultural touchstones. Unclear how much influence it has compared to something like religion, peers, family, etc. 
	**Life-Changing Functions.** 
		Technology is correlated with an increase in obesity. It changed the way we communicate. The downside is that it's difficult to disconnect from constant convenient access to information and people. 

**Narcotizing Dysfunction**
	People are too overwhelmed with media input to really care about the issue, so their involvement becomes defined by awareness instead of by action.

**Gatekeeping**
	The sorting process by which thousands of possible messages are shaped into a mass media-appropriate form and reduced to a manageable amount.

**Panoptic Surveillance**
