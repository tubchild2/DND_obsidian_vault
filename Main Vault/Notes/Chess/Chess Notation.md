
**PIECES**
- K: King
- Q: Queen
- R: Rook
- B: Bishop
- N: Knight
- No Abbreviation: Pawn

**COUNTING**
	The first move is move 1, etc.

**COORDINATES**
![[img coords.png|340]]

White Perspective
	X: A-H
	Y: 1-8

Black Perspective
	X: H-A
	Y: 8-1

**SPECIAL CASES**
Captures
	lowercase x between the piece and the square where the capture occurs
	pawn captures start with the column letter the pawn was on

Castling Kingside
	0-0

Castling Queenside
	0-0-0

Check
	+ at the end of the move

Checkmate
	\# at the end of the move

Game Endings
	1-0 if white wins
	0-1 if black wins
	1/2-1/2 if they draw

**EXAMPLE WITH FOOLS MATE**
1. f3 e5
2. g4 Qh4#
3. 1-0
