**Origins**
- Father belongs to a race of shapeshifting stardust creatures we'll temporarily call Cee
- The lifecycle of Cee begin with the impregnation of another species and the birth of a hybrid
- The hybrid resembles the other species almost entirely, but slowly begins to develop Cee traits over time
- Cee live for 220 years + the lifespan of the hybrid's core species. 
- By the end of their lives, they develop fully into Cee and father more children on other planets, repeating the cycle
- The hero who we'll temporarily call Olan is half human on his mother's side, half Cee on his father's side
- Olan's father died, leaving him with his abusive mother, who he may have accidentally killed in self-defense when he discovered his abilities, causing the mental health struggles he has to this day. 


**Powers**
- The dust-like substance that Cee are made of permeates Olan's cells
- The substance obeys his mental commands, allowing him to use it to reinforce and move it at will. It does not obey instinct, and only functions when specifically willed to. 
- He can protect himself against acceleration and become functionally invincible by fortifying his bones, organs, and skin
- He can move and accelerate any part of his body insanely fast with an insane amount of force, but he cannot process information faster than a human can
- As a consequence of these two abilities, he has
	- Flight
	- Invincibility
	- Super Speed
	- Super Strength
- The substance can exit his body, and it regenerates over time. However, leeching it from his body created billions of micro-lacerations and tears apart his cells, bones, and organs in the process. It's incredibly dangerous and painful. 
- Once outside of his body though, he can use it to create constructs. He'd need a lot of it to do this effectively though, and it's dangerous to get even a small amount. 
- As he grows older though, and he begins to resemble a Cee in nature, he'll become almost entirely made of the substance. At this point, he'll have
	- Constructs
	- Intangibility
	- Shapeshifting


**Mental Health**
- Olan was abused and neglected as a child and experienced intense traumatic experiences. This, in addition to horrendous luck, caused him to develop a suite of mental health disorders. 
	- Amnesic Possessive Dissociative Identity Disorder. Olan has multiple personalities that all front as "Olan," and all have unique memories, behaviors, and interests. They do not remember what the others have done, which causes them to appear to black out and wake up somewhere new at random. 
		- He does have one alter that was originally his primary self. He carries the trauma of what happened when Olan was a child, and keeps it from him. He isn't malicious, he's just very serious and doesn't like to interact with our Olan.
	- Asynchronous Unihemispheric Sleep Disorder. This causes parts of his brain to sleep at different times, allowing him to stay up for extremely long periods without sleeping, though his behavior changes. Each part comes with its own personalities as a consequence of his DID. 
	- Narcolepsy. His brain will rapidly cycle which part of his mind is sleeping, causing him to change who's fronting much faster. It doesn't appear like normal narcolepsy. 
- His abilities are heavily tied to his mental state and are a metaphor for his self-loathing. To use his powers, he needs to learn to cope with and manage his mental health problems. He can't get rid of them, but he can keep them in check without using them as an excuse to hate himself. 


**Stories**
- Acceptance. Accepting that he has these conditions, that he isn't perfect, that he isn't normal, and that it's ok. He accepts who his alters are, and what they want too. 
- Determination. Refusal to give in to his darkest impulses and to stop trying to fix things. 
- Selflessness. Doing what is right even if your life sucks and you don't have to. Like Spider-Man, but instead of things being bad because he doesn't help, things are bad regardless, but he chooses to help out of virtue alone. He doesn't have to, and no one expects him to, but he does anyways because he can. 
- Generational Trauma. Choosing not to do what his father and mother did when he eventually becomes a father.
