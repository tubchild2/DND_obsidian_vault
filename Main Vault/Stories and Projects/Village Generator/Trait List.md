Wealth Chart
Start with 100 wealth +- rand(1-25)

| Wealth Amount | Meaning       |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| -50           | Major Setback |
| -25           | Setback       |
| -10           | Minor Setback |
| 0             | No Effect     |
| 10            | Minor Win     |
| 25            | Win           |
| 50            | Major Win     |

\- means that the village needs to pass this year on the timeline to proc it
\+ means that the village can proc this at any time past this year
< means that the village can only proc this before this time


Traits generate wealth per turn ranging from their min to their max
Whenever a trait is acquired, its cost is subtracted from the village's wealth
