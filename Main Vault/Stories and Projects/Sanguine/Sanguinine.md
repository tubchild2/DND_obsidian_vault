**Physical Properties**
- Lustrous red with black streaks. Produces brilliant red plasma.
- Conductive
- Soft metal. Highly Malleable / Ductile
- 8g/cm^3
- 1600 C melting point

**Acquisition**
- 0.004 ppm of the Earth's crust exclusively in the impact zone
- Mined 400-4000ft below the surface
- Can be found in small fragments in the desert sands
- Miners are required to wear full body suits to prevent accidental explosions on contact and cave-ins. 

**Laws**
1. Sanguinine constantly produces and stores an amount of electricity proportional to the size and purity of the node.
2. If a creature's skin touches a node of Sanguinine, that creature gains full mental control of the electrical output of the node, given no other creature is already in contact with the node, and that they maintain physical contact.  
3. Sanguinine will not output electricity without a mental command to do so. 
4. If directed into the air, the electrical output of Sanguinine will create an accelerated plasma. 
5. The accelerated plasma can range a number of feet equal to ten times the grade of the source node.

**Rough Guidelines**
To use these tables, find the grade of the node by summing its size and purity grade additives, and use the wattage table to find out its suggested output. If you have multiple nodes, just write them as 
3x III + 2x IV etc.

| Size     | Purity   | Purification Method   | Grade Additive |
| -------- | -------- | --------------------- | -------------- |
| Grain    | <75%     | Poor Quality Raw      | +0             |
| Pebble   | >75%     | Decent Quality Raw    | +1             |
| Stone    | >90%     | Smelting              | +2             |
| Boulder  | >99%     | Electrolytic Refining | +3             |
| Megalith | >99.9%   | Chemical Purification | +4             |
| Hoard    | >99.999% | Perfect Purification  | +5             |

| Grade | Comparable Wattage           | Comparable Destructive Potential |

**Examples**
Lower Class Technology
- Grade I-III
- Used by outlaws and poor people in dusty mining towns
- Primarily uses small impure fragments
- Superheated knives, basic pistols, cherry bombs
- Looks like Tony Stark's tech in the cave

Middle Class Technology
- Grade IV-V
- Used by regular everyday people in small cities or in the ruin cities
- Has access to reasonably sized and basically purified nodes
- Guns, knives, swords, clubs, maces, shurikens, Sanguinine mag-lift public transit systems, automation / factories, cosmetics / keepsakes, etc.
- Looks like 1800s Cowboy tech

Upper Class Technology
- Grade VI-VII
- Used by the richest of the rich, primarily those affiliated with the Needle King. 
- Uses the biggest, purest nodes with the best engineering in the known world
- Laser cannons, exosuits, fire walls, super-powered hand cannons, bombs, missiles, vehicles, RPGs, and more. 
- Looks like nightmare fuel if it comes from the Needle King. Otherwise, it's usually bigger, meaner, and made of metal. 

Mythical Technology
- Grade VIII-X
- The stuff of legends. 
- Maybe one or two nodes of this quality or size exist in the whole world. 
- Town levelling weapons and lasers
