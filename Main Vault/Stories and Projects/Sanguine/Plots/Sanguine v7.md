In this version, I'm redoing the worldbuilding. Here's the basics:

#### LORE
- Some kind of genetic apocalypse happened turning the world into a high desert
- The survivors of that apocalypse were the Ferromancers
- Ferromancers can manipulate electricity and magnetism and that's it
- The Red Culling is an underground criminal network of the remaining Ferromancers. They hunt down and kill anyone with Ferromantic potential, and operate almost entirely in secret. They're led by the Needle King, and his right hand man Inquisitor Primus. 
- There are multiple large settlements across the map, but the biggest is a Detroit-style noir city called Vata.


#### PLOT OVERVIEW
In this world, cruelty is encouraged and rewarded; the dominant business in the world is piracy and scavenging. The entire world is split into a web of outlaw territories and criminal networks. 

Mell Delaney is an outlaw with his brother Jesse, whose work attracts the ire of the Red Culling. Mell's father Hank takes the blame for Mell's actions, and is brutally wounded by Inquisitor Primus. The wound festers and causes an infection that's slowly killing Hank. Mell quits and becomes a vigilante. He wants to clean up Vata and the rest of Terra, one outlaw at a time. He isn't above stealing from criminals, but he won't ever kill.  

Mell may not have any superpowers, but he is a gifted engineer and an experienced outlaw. He can design his own weapons using a synthesized power source called Sanguinine. It's a telepathically controlled material that can be used to make all kinds of crazy weapons. 

Mell has self-worth issues, which is part of his drive. He views himself so lowly that he'll push himself to ridiculous points to push up everyone else. It doesn't help that he's done horrible things in his past. 


#### CONFLICTS
###### Faith and Motivation
**Conflict**
	The world is too oppressive for him to realistically make a difference.
	Progress is regularly erased, and he needs to do the same thing over and over again. 
	Multiple of his wins are corrupted at the last minute and aren't satisfying. 
	He can't overcome the Red Culling. Killing ONE inquisitor is as monumental feat. 
	He doesn't have powers, so he wonders if he can really do anything. 
	He doesn't know if any of this is really his responsibility. 
	He has to keep getting back up anyways because it's right, and because no one else will.

**Inquisitor Primus**
	The lead inquisitor of the Red Culling. He's unstoppable, uncatchable, and much more powerful than any of his other enemies. Primus wants to break Mell's spirit by demonstrating the futility of his actions. He's obsessed with his work and is relentless. He's almost impossible to track, and you'd better pray to god he doesn't get help from any other Inquisitors. He has a lean metal suit with a tall mask and a cloak. He wields a thin, needle-like sword. 

**Clifford (Cliff) Foster**
	Cliff is a young Ferromancer discovered by Primus. Cliff refused to join the culling, so Primus killed his family, wounded Cliff, and has been hunting him ever since. Mell sees Cliff as a son, and he becomes one of the few people he reveals his identity to. He cooks him food, teaches him how to be stealthy, and he helps him use his powers to their fullest. 
	Primus is so relentless that it becomes evident that there's no way for Mell to keep Cliff from him forever. Mell has to send Cliff away to somewhere no one will ever follow him, something that breaks both of their hearts. Cliff refuses, and gets killed by Primus anyways, and Mell blames himself for it. 

**Wendell Barnes (Public Persona)**
	An attorney who prosecutes the people Mell saves and makes a ton of money doing it. He exploits the legal system like Saul Goodman, and actively helps the criminals Mell puts away get out. Perpetuating the cycle of crime in the city gives him a never ending stream of revenue. He has a gigantic law firm, and lives in an enormous tower in the center of the city that's guarded by a private army. All he wants is to create more criminals, because the more chaos exists in the city, the more powerful he becomes. He is the law. He wants to make sure everything Mell does is undone as soon as possible. 

**Dr. Elliot Cassidy**
	Him and Mell are both good hearted people who believe in trying to make the world a better place. They are both struggling with the idea that you can't make a difference without power. Mell gets a job working for him developing a way to awaken latent mutations from the calamity. Pre-calamity humans had access to gene editing, making them (on average), several feet taller, multiple times stronger and faster, and incredibly durable. When his work is bought by the city for military applications, Cassidy uses it on himself and becomes a vigilante. Unfortunately, he sees a more direct path to liberation than Mell does, and starts killing a bunch of criminals. What Mell comes to realize is that you can make a difference without power. If you use your power to force a way of life onto people, it doesn't work. You need patience. He's a dark mirror of Mell; just as intelligent, with the same motives, but far more powerful and crazy. 

**Hank Delaney**
	Hank was wounded by Inquisitor Primus and developed an unidentifiable terminal infection. Modern medicine can only slow what's coming. Hank and Mell have a very close relationship, so it's been very painful to watch. Hank has started lying to Mell about his condition to give him false hope, and has been hiding medical reports and his coughs. Mell knows what he's doing. He's a very loving old man who encourages Mell to continue doing what is right. Hank is one of the only people that knows what Mell is doing, though he keeps this to himself. He wants Mell to keep going. 

**Jesse Delaney**
	Jesse resents Mell for leaving his life as an outlaw. He's ruining his life, missing payments for Hank, and nearly dying in the process for something he can't talk about. He wants to take care of his own, and is frustrated with Mell keeping secrets from him. He won't attack Mell, but he will stoop to low places to try and make him confess and quit. When he learns the truth, he still disagrees, but is more understanding. His involvement and desire to protect Mell puts him in danger, and may get him killed. 


###### Guilt and Pride
**Conflict**
	Guilt weighs heavily on his soul whenever he does anything for himself.
	Mell has to make horrible, impossible decisions that ruin lives. He can't save everyone.
	Some innocent people are harmed as a biproduct of his vigilantism. 
	Mell has self-hate tendencies, he thinks he's a terrible person, and every mistake feels monumental to him. 
	Mell has horrendous anxiety, depressive tendencies, and is prone to impulsive decision making. 
	He feels great about the good he's doing. 
	He helps kids, the elderly, the disabled, the depressed, and more. 
	He saves lives from gangs, outlaws, disasters, and more. 
	He puts an end to all of the really big and bad names in crime. 
	He inspires people to do more.

**Daisy Mackland**
	When he was younger, Mell did some horrible things. The worst thing he ever did was kidnapping Mary Mackland. She knew too much, and if she had left the building Mell, his brother, and his whole gang would've been discovered and killed. Mell isn't a killer, so he dragged it out for way too long and got close to her. He tried to make a deal with her, but he knew she wouldn't take it. She ended up dying, and Mell was complicit in it. It was the worst day of Mell's life by far. 
	Daisy Mackland, her older sister, rightfully hates Mell. She doesn't just want revenge, she wants to make Mell understand how bad of a person he is. She especially despises this whole noble hero persona he's putting on, since it's incredibly hypocritical. 
	In her pursuit of Mell, she visited the site of the calamity. There's a section of the ruins that no one ever returns from, and she entered, hoping to find the source of Ferromancy, or something better. Whatever the calamity was, it screwed with local spacetime. 
	Daisy's contact with the source granted her limited control over space and perception. Given no one is observing and the space manipulation is seamless, she can make walls and doorways appear, manipulate space, create impossible geometry, and screw with people's heads. 
	She presents moral quandaries to Mell to test him, often in brutal ways. She's completely obsessed with him. She makes him see things using his powers, and manufactures scenarios to test him. She dresses like a ghost in a white suit to represent purity of the soul. She never kills, but she does find more creative ways to harass people. Once she feels she's proven his point, she'll rest, but that seems unlikely, since she keeps returning to taunt him.
	She makes Mell confront his self-hate and horrible past. She questions if people can really change or redeem themselves. Mell admits he's done terrible things and doesn't try to make himself look better than he is. 
	As she continues to appear, she becomes more and more mysterious in nature. She apparently starts learning and seeing this with her powers that are way beyond us. She becomes a bit more benevolent. 

**Martha Delaney**
	Mell's alcoholic mother. She blames Mell for Hank's wounds because his mistakes as an outlaw are what attracted Inquisitor Primus to them in the first place. Hank took the fall for Mell and barely survived. Whenever Mell pushes back, Martha plays the victim. She resents him for not paying more and being there for them, even though she isn't herself. 


###### Health and Comfort
**Conflict**
	His job with Dr. Cassidy (pre-mutation) is awful, doesn't pay much, and is in constant jeopardy.
	Almost all of his money comes from stealing from the people he's putting away. 
	Unfortunately, his gear and medical bills are extremely expensive and are taking all of that money. 
	He lacks a comfortable amount of money, food, water, and shelter. 
	He's exhausted from a lack of quality sleep (or sleep in general). 
	He is regularly beaten senseless and horrifically injured. His body is in bad shape. 
	He has pre-existing health conditions. 
	He wants to quit and use his money on himself, but he can't. 

**Wendell Barnes (Criminal Side)**
	Barnes was originally a good hearted man who put several horrible men behind bars, but he quit and started double dealing because he wanted to start a family with a more comfortable amount of money. He is much more involved in perpetuating crime and corruption in the city than originally thought. He's laundering money, he's protecting identities, he's manufacturing gang wars, it's horrible. He's a kingpin. Though he is a bad person, the questions he asks are much more complicated. He questions if helping people was ever his responsibility. He questions if wanting to take care of yourself is really selfish. He attacks Mell's entitlement and desire for self-care.

**Burton Bowler**
	Burton Bowler's parents lost custody of his when he was 7, and he was later placed in the care of his abusive grandmother. Because of genetic misfortune and his traumatic childhood, he developed a combination of possessive dissociative identity disorder, narcolepsy, and something brand new called asynchronous unihemispheric sleep disorder. So, in addition to switching alters normally, falling asleep forces another alter front until they fall asleep or recede. 
	Burton is the host. He's introverted, selfless, and quiet. He keeps to himself and his hobbies and lives alone. He's constantly exhausted and fighting the urge to fall asleep. He has to nurse wounds he wakes up with, go to work, and live a normal life. 
	Alter 2 is a persecutor-protector. All he wants is to protect Burton and prepare him for more traumatic experiences. He'll front whenever Burton is threatened emotionally, physically, or verbally, he'll hurt himself to punish Burton / prepare him for future abuse, and he'll obsessively exercise, eat, and clean himself. He's selfish, stubborn, and aggressive. He's known to go on holy moral missions to protect Burton, which can (in semi-rare cases) involve criminal activity. 
	Burton is already struggling to manage this. Thankfully, neither of them are powerful enough to do anything drastic. Well, they were. One day, Burton acquires superpowers. He can conjure and manipulate a functionally unlimited amount of energy. That means that he has telekinesis, flight, invulnerability, super strength, super speed, and more. The only drawback is that it's only active when he wants it to be. If he isn't actively trying to protect himself, he's just a guy. 
	Now that Alter 2 has power, he can start talking some sense into the people exploiting Burton. The problem is, he's going a bit too far and planning things that could hurt people. He won't kill, but that still leaves a lot of cruelty on the table. Burton has to suppress his powers and his alter, which means staying awake and staying away from perceivable threats. This is making Alter 2 punish him harder and lash out even more. It's a vicious cycle. If Alter 2 gets his hands on someone that hurt them when they were younger like he wants, he may do something he'll regret. He can be talked down because he's not insane like many depictions of DID.
	His lack of self-care for the greater good mirrors Mell's story, and the solution to their problems is more nuanced than just give up on what you want for others. Burton can't destroy Alter 2 in the same way that Mell can't ignore himself. They both need to take care of themselves. The goal is to rehabilitate Alter 2 and to be reasonable about taking care of oneself.  
	After the story, Burton and his alter become much more free about interchangeably fronting at will. 

**Ruth Faro**
	Mell meets a broker named Ruth Faro. She won't explain anything about herself, or show her face, but she can get him bounty hunting work and leads. She gets him a lot of information and payment in exchange for him helping her capture / sabotage some of her enemies. It's a questionable compromise. 

**Philip Baskin**
	Mr. Baskin owns the building Mell lives in, as well as the building Martha and Hank live in. He's a greedy, selfish, and callous man. He'll threaten eviction over missed payments and keeps finding new reasons to charge them. Though greedy, he'll occasionally listen to reason if things get really bad. Inspired by Mr. Ditkovich. 


###### Love and Loneliness
**Conflict**
	He wants deep personal connections over everything else, and he can never have them. 
	His relationship with his girlfriend Marion is slowly and painfully choked out.
	His brutally wounded father Hank is dying and treatments are too expensive for him to cover anymore. 
	His brother Jesse resents him for not taking care of himself or his father. 
	His endangers the lives of any new friends he makes as a vigilante. 
	His alcoholic mother blames Mell for Hank's injuries and is abusive. 
	He feels isolated. 

**Hank Delaney**
	Mell and Hank are very close, and he tries to be there for him as much as he can. One of the most painful parts of this whole process is having to spend so much time away from him. Hank keeps encouraging him to continue doing what he's doing, but Mell doesn't want to if it means losing time with his father. 

**Martha Delaney**
	Martha is an alcoholic who's spending all her money on whiskey, and very little on Hank. She expects pity from her children because of her condition, so she expects them to cover all of Hank's expenses. Despite everything she does, Mell still loves her, and remembers the woman she used to be before all of this. He still covers her with a blanket when she passes out, and they still sometimes have breakfast together. He's trying his hardest to repair their broken relationship, but he doesn't have the time for it. 

**Jesse Delaney**
	Mell wants to quit and spend time with his brother. 

**Marion Miller**
	Marion is an innocent bystander. Her and Mell had a wholesome relationship before Hank's injury, and there's nothing wrong with them wanting to pursue each other. Mell's secret double-life and her suspicions begin slowly choking their relationship. Mell has to break her heart, which is completely unfair to her. Marion is a sweet girl who stays in his life as a perpetual temptation, and she knows it. She'll flirt with him, ask him leading questions, and try to get him back. Eventually, she'll move on to someone else and won't shy away from rubbing it in, because Mell is the one who didn't act. 


###### Public Animosity and Wrath
**Conflict**
	Most people hate his hero persona for being counter to local law and the code of honor.  
	He's slandered and used as a scapegoat by the public. People don't want to be associated with him. 
	He's attacked and harassed by an onslaught of rivals, some of which personally target him and go too far. 
	Some people don't want to be saved by him. 
	Innocent people are endangered because of their relationship to him, forcing him to conceal his identity. 
	Those who know his identity go after his friends and family. 
	His morality prevents him from killing these people, which makes people hate him more. 
	Mell wants to kill people, and each mistake he makes pushes the public further away from him. 

**Jeffery Reeves**
	Reeves origins are unclear. He's a mercenary that has a healing factor, and fights with his bare hands. He's a very methodical and intelligent killer. He suggests he was abused or taken advantage of a lot as a child, causing him to develop the kill-or-be-killed / me first mentality the rest of the world has, except taken to an extreme degree. He's shrouded in mystery. 
	He doesn't like Mell because of what he stands for. He views Mell as a weak willed person that's too afraid of what people think about him. The world is a dark place, and you need to take what you can get. You can't slow yourself down for everyone else because god knows they won't do it for you. He attacks Mell's morality. 

**Mayor Beatrice Tackett**
	Mayor Tackett is a horrible human being with no one in her life that has people killed if she thinks they deserve it. Her brutality gets clean results, and she's executed countless horrible people. She's adored by the public. She constantly slanders and blames Mell for her own mistakes. She portrays him as a monster that could be around any corner. One that's causing people to kill each other. She attacks his reputation any way she can, and tries to have him arrested and killed.

**Sister Maya (May) Ehlert**
	A medic for a local church, and an activist. Helps to take care of the people Mell injures. She's disgusted by his violent actions and encourages a more pacifistic approach based on her religion (which is a modified version of Catholocism). She advocates for his arrest. Mell will volunteer in his free time to help at the church, but he's been unable to convince her that he's helpful.
