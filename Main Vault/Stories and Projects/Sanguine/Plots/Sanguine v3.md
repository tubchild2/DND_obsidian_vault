
#### Overview
The world of [[SANGUINE OVERVIEW]] is built up in a way to demonstrate how cruelty is rewarded. 

**THEME**
Cruelty is rewarded in this world, and being a hero is hard.  
Cruelty breeds more cruelty, and kindness breeds more kindness. 
Being nice makes you feel good. 

**ROUGH PLOT**
Melvin (Mell) Delaney begins the story with a kill or be killed / "desperate times" type philosophy. 
He's a high-ranking outlaw in the dark underworld of Terra. 
He is a good person at heart, who doesn't enjoy his cold blooded but wealthy lifestyle. 
His older brother Jesse is also a [[Ferromancy|Ferromancer]], and has a similar job. They enjoy a very close bond.
Mell's best friend Cliff is sweeter than grandma's cookies and not affiliated with the outlaw life. 
Jesse is objective, detached, and isn't above killing. He's greedy, but he has loving relationships. 

Mell and Jesse learn through their work that Cliff has a target on his back. 
To save Cliff, Mell risks his own life and anonymity as a [[Ferromancy|Ferromancer]]. 
Cliff is killed anyways. 

Mell hunts down the killer and attempts to get his revenge. 
Mell's life as an outlaw indirectly caused the killer (who's affiliated with the Red Culling) to go after Cliff. 
Mell understands what he's been doing to people, how it feels, and the cyclical nature of cruelty. 

Jesse attempts to console him, but his slightly detached and pragmatic views are slightly insulting.  
Mell feels guilty for what happened and wants revenge, something Jesse agrees to help him get. 
They get revenge, and it feels good. 

They start going after other gangs and criminals instead of the innocent.
They're killing a lot of really bad people on their crusade.
Jesse appreciates spoils and aggressive expansion, while Mell appreciates helping people.
This reaches a breaking point when Mell does something risky to save someone for no reason. 

Mell doesn't want to be a hero just for what he gets out of it, and for revenge. 
Mell is a genuinely good person at heart. 
Being nice makes him feel good and be able to sleep at night. 
Jesse disagrees. He has a more practical view of the situation.
If they start acting like Robin Hood, they'll get the Culling sent after them and they'll get themselves and people important to them killed. 
They can go after people who deserve it, and they'll be rewarded for it, but they have to draw the line somewhere. Jesse knows that the life Mell envisions isn't realistic, and it'd be unbearable and suicide.
Mell argues that, as [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]], they have the power to make a real difference. He feels a calling, but Jesse just doesn't. 
Mell quits the gang to pursue this. 

His life of heroism begins to take its toll and ruin his life
His actions as a vigilante have attracted the attention of the Red Culling.
His relationship with his brother is seriously strained. 
Getting enough money to get by becomes nigh impossible.
On the bright side, he's becoming a MUCH better [[Ferromancy|Ferromancer]]. 
ONE inquisitor is sent after him and Jesse, and they nearly die and are forced on the run. 

Their living situation is terrible, and he's eating and drinking garbage. 
Mell and Jesse both start practicing [[Ferromancy]] to prepare for the next Inquisitor encounter.
Jesse rubs it in that this is literally exactly what he said would happen. 
Mell wants to quit, and Jesse is pressuring him back to his old ways. 
Mell wonders why he isn't rewarded, why he shouldn't value his own happiness, and why he shouldn't take time to help himself too.
Mell and Jesse's fighting is based almost entirely on principle at this point, and it's getting really bad.
The Inquisitor shows up again, and Jesse and Mell finally split up, since they both want to "protect" the other but can't agree on how.  

I'm not sure how to end the story, but it'll be something along these lines:
- Jesse is successful, and remains as such. He's morally bankrupt, and detached from his family, but he's rich, safe, and has the connections to protect Mell. He isn't really content and is always on the move and his life is constantly stressful. 
- Mell's life sucks, and it's nothing but sacrifices. However, when he's about to quit, the people he's saved begin using his insignia as a symbol of hope, and he's inspired because he gets to see all the people he helped, and that's his reward. Mell's acts of kindness have inspired more kindness, and that makes Mell happy. Really happy. 
- Maybe for the climax Mell defeats the Inquisitor, and I could use the Culling as a motif for greed or callousness in the story and this could be his big triumphant moment. Him actually killing one is an insane feat since they're terrifying. 

Later Stories
- Mell and Jesse don't fight since they're ideological rivals but they're still siblings that have each other's backs. That'd be like a MUCH later thing if that were to happen. 
- Jesse gets obsessive about his work and starts to push people away in pursuit of happiness through comfort and security, but it's all surface level and he's depressed and anxious all the time. 
- Mell's life gets comically bad in every way I can come up with, but he's happy because of the good effect he's having on the world, and the bad effect he's having on the Culling's overbearing control.
- The Needle King gets more involved in tormenting Mell personally. He'll indoctrinate Jesse into the culling over time, and tempt Mell to do the same. He's an accelerant to both of their arcs, and also represents the absolute maximum success that comes from being a terrible person. 
- Mell's followers start protesting the Culling, which forms riots, which forms a Pinkerton like gang, which leads to a small rebellion, which Mell gets involved with. His primary goal is safety, and he doesn't want anyone else to be involved in pushing back against the Culling. This forms an interesting moral quandary since without their help, his odds of really making any BIG difference is low, but with them, he's all but guaranteeing the deaths of countless genuine, innocent people that *he* inspired, all for a rebellion that A, he doesn't know will work, and B, hinges on an image of himself that he doesn't know if he can live up to. 
- Mell occasionally dips back into the dark side, but he defeats the Needle King in the end.
