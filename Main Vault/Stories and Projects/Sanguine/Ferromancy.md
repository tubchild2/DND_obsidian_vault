**Early Gene Editing**
Pre-impact society commonly practiced gene editing. As an embryo, children are equipped with passive genes to physically enhance them. It was an extensive editing process, but the notable edits were less myostatin, better telomere maintenance, denser bones, more hemoglobin, much greater myofibril packing density, much stronger joints, ligaments, and tendons, and several major skeletal tweaks to accommodate (among other things) enhanced lung size. Essentially, everyone would be stronger, faster, healthier, and live longer. The editing extended to their reproductive system to allow them to pass these genes onto their children. 

After some years, a pharmaceutical company produced and sold an epigenetic serum to bring out the full potential of the genes. Over the course of several years of exposure, those who took the serum were 6-10 times stronger, weighed 2 times more, were 2 times as durable, were 2 times as fast, and lived nearly 2 times as long. Post-impact, the serum was lost, as was the only way to really access the full potential of those genes. 


**Acquisition**
Roughly 0.4% of newborns post-impact are born with the Ferromantic mutation due to exposure to [[Sanguinine]] in the mother's blood while in the womb. If one parent is a Ferromancer, the odds increase to 4%. If both parents are Ferromancers, it's a 40% chance.

Those with the Ferromantic mutation experienced a rare reaction to [[Sanguinine]], in which it had a similar impact on the body as the pre-impact epigenetic serum and fully incorporated itself into the body. This grants them enhanced strength, speed, endurance, and a suite of electrical abilities as a consequence of the [[Sanguinine]] being so deeply incorporated into their bodies. Ferromancers can create and manipulate electricity and magnetic fields. They aren't immune to electrocution, though, nor do they have any enhanced resistance to it. 

Ferromancers utilize the [[Sanguinine]] in their blood much more efficiently than any machine ever could.


**Ferromantic Potential**
The more [[Sanguinine]] you have in your blood, the greater your Ferromantic potential. Your Ferromantic potential will not change unless you inject [[Sanguinine]] into your blood. This action does not grant the mutation, and will typically result in burns, poisoning, or death. Ferromantic potential is passed unto children, give or take 10-25%.

While your potential is unchanged, your ability to fully take advantage of the mutation requires both regular exercise and skill. It typically takes ~4 years of perfect daily training to reach your maximum power. As you progress, your body will mutate and you will gain enhanced control over the [[Sanguinine]]. Notably, your size does not increase as much as your muscle density, allowing for Ferromancers to have a shockingly lean build while weighing hundreds of additional pounds. The activation of the genes from the gene editing also keep you younger and healthier for far longer than normal.

Being in contact with external [[Sanguinine]] grants Ferromancers access to the energy produced by the node. This provides a quick way to produce and manipulate plasma without needing an absurdly high Ferromantic potential. That's why they line their weapons and armor with it. However, they are not invincible to lightning nor to the damage it causes to the body. Having extremely powerful electric currents pass through your body regardless of how well you can control them hurts very bad. Ferromancers use lightning redirection as self-defense only.

**Ferromancy in-Universe**
Those born with Ferromantic potential who reveal their abilities are hunted down and indoctrinated by the Needle King. Those who don't side with him are killed to prevent them from posing any threat to the Culling and its regime. For this reason, most Ferromancers don't have any reason to push past adept. Some reach Knight, and the Needle King and his hand are some of the only masters out there. 

Inquisitors are typically 6'4", and 270lbs. They're 50% stronger, 40% faster, and 40% more durable. They can lift 8800 pounds of metal, cling to metal surfaces, create small arcs of white-hot plasma, redirect lightning, sense metal, magnetism, and current nearby, and have enhanced reflexes. They wear dark heavy armor, with 4ft needle swords and chains. Teams are made up of needle, caster, tech, and brute variants, typically 2-4 of them, though 1 is more than enough. 

The Needle King has an mgsdl of 9300, putting him on the upper end of Legendary. He's 10'9", and 1440lbs. He's 17x stronger, 2.5 times faster, 2.5 times more durable. He can lift 140,800lbs of metal, levitate, chain lightning bolts, protect himself with a magnetic barrier, and has every other Ferromantic ability. He wears an extremely heavy set of dark, needle lined armor, and wields a 6ft needle sword lined with Grade VI [[Sanguinine]]. His bracers are lined with Grade VI [[Sanguinine]]. He's very old at this point, but his superior genes have kept him strong and sharp.

**Countering a Ferromancer**
The top ways are explosives, poison, crushing, and drowning. You can electrocute them, but to do that you need to catch them off guard with lightning, which is tough to do. You can fight and kill them, but that's even harder to do unless you're in a large team or also a Ferromancer. Trapping them is by far the best way to get rid of an Inquisitor. 


**Styles of Combat**
	Sword Combat
		Favors direct sword combat with the needle.
	Plasma Combat
		Favors use of [[Sanguinine]] equipment to grant the ability to direct and fire plasma. 
	Tech Combat
		Favors a selection of modified [[Sanguinine]] weapons and technology. 
	Physical Combat
		Favors use of the enhanced physical traits to dominate opponents physically.
