### Research
**Functionalist Perspective**
- Cultural norms function to support the fluid operation of society by guiding people in making choices
- Functionalists also pay attention to what the culture values
- The different categories of culture serve many functions, like how membership in a culture brings camaraderie

**Conflict Theorist Perspective**
- Social structure is inherently unequal and based on power differentials like class, gender, race, and age. 
- Less powerful groups are omitted from history
- Cultural norms benefit some but hurt others
- Groups with more material wealth can adapt to cultural change faster

**Symbolic Interactionalist Perspective**
- They believe culture is created and maintained by the ways people interact and in how individuals interpret each other's actions. 
- They believe human interaction is a continuous process of deriving meaning from both objects in the environment and the actions of others. 
- They believe that objects and interactions all have symbolic meaning. 
- Language exists as a means for people to communicate interpretations of these meanings to others. 

**What is culture?**
- The shared values, beliefs, norms / rules of a group of people. 
- They often share a language, create art and artifacts. 
- Some believe culture is the collective identity and memory of a group of people.

### Interview Process
**Subjects**
- Different backgrounds and/or generations

**Process**
- In person / zoom / over the phone
- Ask "what cultural changes have you noticed in your lifetime"
- Take 2 pages of handwritten notes per person
- Follow their lead granted they don't deviate from sociological topics
- Improvise open-ended follow up questions to to keep them on topic
- After the interview, draw a hard line across your notepaper and log
	- Date and time
	- Method of interviewing
	- Subject
	- Location
	- Brief reflection
		- How did the interview feel?
		- What moment or comment do you remember most clearly?
		- Were there any issues or distractions?
		- Was the data easy to collect?
