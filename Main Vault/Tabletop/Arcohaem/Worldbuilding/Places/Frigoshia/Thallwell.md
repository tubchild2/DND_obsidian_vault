Just outside New Durkheim, in the west wood of Thallwell, rests a cliff sheet of ice rock. Scaling it leads to a small grove surrounding a chasm. A small hut with a weathered chimney. Smoke is pluming out of it, and the warm glow of a fireplace can be seen through the dirty brown windows. 

*What do you do?*
(Investigating chasm)
	It's fenced off. A large wooden sign has "keep out" painted on it in yellow. There's a gate locked with a rudimentary lock. 
	(Hopping fence or opening gate)
		STEALTH CHECK
		Stepping towards the chasm, it appears to be a normal cave that happens to go surprisingly deep. There are a bunch of weird plants growing out of it that you don't recognize. 
			(Investigating plants)
				STEALTH CHECK
				It looks like a garden of crazy prehistoric plants. Some flourish in crazy shapes, sizes, and colors, some grow geometric roots and melt the snow, and others look like mockeries of people; imitating faces, bodies, and breathing. 
				None of them look like they should grow here. 
				They don't even really look like they should be from earth. 
				(Interacting with them)
					STEALTH CHECK
					Roll 1d4

						DC16 CON. You lean down and are hit with an incredibly powerful, sweet stench. You feel the air move into your body, and you instantly feel lightheaded.

						This plant looks like a black pillar of coal with small holes in it. Getting closer, it feels like it's burning something inside of it, like a tiny natural furnace.

						DC16 WIS. You lean down and spot a large flower with a series of concentric pink and blue circles in it. As you get closer, it starts flashing blinding lights at you, causing you to feel dizzy.

**PLANTS**
Fumewort
	Looks like a very small barren tree with yellow holes in it. 
	DC16 CON save to resist 1hr of delirium from the sweet smell. 

Fernbore
	Looks like a black pillar of coal with small ember filled holes in it. 
	It's a tiny natural furnace that's spitting out black ashen seeds. 
	It consumes heat instead of sunlight. 
	Extremely sticky; almost impossible to remove from biological matter. 

Angel Bud
	Looks like a large flower with a series of concentric pink and blue circles on each petal. 
	Grows hypotrochoidal roots and large green berries. 
	DC16 WIS save to resist 30 seconds of paralysis as it flashes blinding colorful lights. 

Ichorocyst
	Looks like a blue cactus with orange needles that are dripping a viscous yellow substance.

###### What a Dunce
Grayson Bordges is getting bullied by some kids. They're making fun of him for his mental disabilities, and they mock his voice and shove him into a wall and call him useless. He just sits there and takes it. He already had a black eye.

He can't read, write, or do math. He's not particularly strong and his dad (Trey) is disappointed in him. His mom Dierdre, sides with Trey but is sweet to him behind Trey's back. His dad hits him to punish him to try and toughen him up like how his father did. 

He's really sweet though! He has a crush on Izzie Meaden, who lives across the street, but he can't work up the courage to ask her out. He gave her a flower when she was crying once. 

His brother Tommy got lost in the woods in West Thallwell / East New Durkehim and never returned. He was 13, and it's been a few days, so they're putting up missing posters. The party will likely run into these posters and recall a certain other Tommy that shouldn't have played in the woods. 

Grayson really wants to help them, and if they do anything, he'll notice that they're new in town so he'll hand draw them a map with crayon and a note that says thank you. It's not very useful, but it'll hopefully stick with them

Kaul does not support this mission as it's an unnecessary risk.
