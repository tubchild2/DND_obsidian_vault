### Quests
###### Turf War
THALLWELL OR LEISTUNGBOURG

**PeleCO in Thallwell**
The farm kids in Thallwell formed a lemonade stand empire called PeleCo. Pele is a Frigoshian word for juice. They were led by Little Timmy Contos, the son of Lord Coby Contos (the Trapper) and Vivian Contos. Timmy is a clever, street wise kid who has Saul Goodman style tactics to get ahead of those more advantaged than him. He's been spending his entire *allowance* to fund the business because he his friends want more money to buy food. Little Timmy's last name will be kept secret. 

His Uncle Randy Contos is very present in his life since his father keeps going on work trips and drinks a lot. He's a good influence. He's Vivian's brother. 

They know Grayson Bordges. They don't really like him. 

**Saft in Leistungbourg**
The middle-class and rich kids in Leistungbourg saw PeleCO's success in the community and wanted to steal it so they could have money to buy toys. So, they opened "Saft," which is also a Frigoshian word for juice. They used their expanded resources and free time to absolutely dominate them. 

They're led by Lee Lamberson, son of Caroline and Merve Lamberson. He's a chubby german boy with cowlicked blond hair, big red cheeks, and a button nose. He's filthy rich, manipulative, entitled, and beloved by the public. He's a horrendously evil child. 

**The Conflict**
Saft started sending people to attack PeleCO's stands to claim stand territories, and they're paying kids to stand guard and make snowballs. One kid is crying because his stand was destroyed and he doesn't have enough money to help buy his mom food. 

The PCs can intervene and settle the conflict however they choose.
