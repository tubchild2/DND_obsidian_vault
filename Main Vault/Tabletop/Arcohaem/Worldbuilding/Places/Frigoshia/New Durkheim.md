#### Quests
**MAXWELL'S SILVER HAMMER**
A piece of folklore tied to Torhild. There was once a man who hated his brother who was a carpenter. He was a master ethologist, so one day, he cursed his brother's hammer to bring bad luck. But, it went wrong. His hatred messed it up, and made his brother extra lucky. So, he tried again and again, but still, his hatred prevented it from working. So, finally, when he got fed up, he put one final binding on it to make it only target his brother. But, it still didn't work. Furious, he tried to use the hammer himself, and seven days later, he slipped on a puddle and broke his neck in the town square. The moral of the story is not to try and be vindictive, but the hammer really did exist. His brother was really lucky with it, but once he died, it became a legendary item because anyone who used it or was hit by it would have something really bad happen to them in 7 days. It's out there somewhere, waiting to be found. Some believe it was passed down by the brother, but no one knows who it was. 


**THE CONQUEROR**
A fencer and strategist. He was rejected from the army because of a previous injury. Now he sits in the park challenging people to chess and gloating about how great he could've been. He has a small group of fans that like watching him kick the pants off other cocky people. He wants an equal. Once he gets one (Tinkle Winkler probably), he'll get obsessed with learning more about them. He'll call them an artist and develop a strong friendship.
