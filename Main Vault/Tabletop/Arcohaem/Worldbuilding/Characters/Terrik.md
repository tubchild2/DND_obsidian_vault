### History
**Part 1 - Origins**
Terrik Malvek was born in Hephaestus in 1221. His mother and father were bridge builders for the city, and they made next to nothing. They regularly abused Terrik and blamed him for ruining their relationship and for being too expensive to care for. His father had an array of disorders and addictions that made his behavior unpredictable and irrational. His mother was a deeply spiteful woman who would enter depressive states when anyone did anything minor to her, often causing his father to become extra upset with them. 

Terrik would punish himself for doing anything sub-optimally, causing him to develop chronic self-worth issues. He would enter self-destructive states where he'd act out and get into fights because he felt he was already too far gone. 

In 1227, his younger brother Otto was born. He got it worse than Terrik did, and was completely neglected by their parents. Terrik essentially raised Otto. Their days of playing on the streets were made better by their neighbor Ingrid, who Terrik had a crush on. 

Terrik did terribly in school and was regularly called stupid by his peers. He stole a comic book out of a dumpster and read about the heroes of the Republic military, who were off fighting battles all across Serendipita. He wanted to leave home and be a hero, so, for the next several years, becoming the greatest hero in Serendipita became his one and only fixation. 


**Part 2 - Something Better**
The day he was 18, in 1239, he registered for military service and got in. He trained until his bones cracked day after day. He was so relentless and efficient that he managed to outwit and overpower his entire squadron in a training exercise. 

While this was happening, his brother Otto started a business selling comics, and married Ingrid. A few years later, in 1244, they adopted a daughter from a poor household named Abelia. They spoke sometimes, but it was rare. Otto seemed so happy, but Terrik just wasn't. He'd become so obsessed with his image that he would regularly stay up for days at a time, saddled with guilt and self-hate. He felt that he didn't make his parents proud, that people secretly disliked him, and that he was worse than everyone else. 

In 1245, both of his parents passed away. He knew they were bad parents that had mental health issues, but he was still devastated that they died not proud of him. In their will, Terrik was excluded from majority of their possessions for "abandoning them." This was in spite of them constantly wishing that he'd leave them, and him functionally raising Otto for them for 12 years. 

His career was doing quite well, though. By 1249, he'd earned the rank of captain. In hopes of making himself appear more impressive than he was, he'd buried himself in clothes, mansions, drinks, art, and so on. He didn't believe he deserved anything, causing him to develop an unbelievably temperamental ego. He was highly competitive, had multiple assault charges, and had developed an addiction to a performance enhancing drug called Redline. Every time he tried to make friends or to get a partner, he'd self-destruct and tell himself he didn't deserve it, leading him to bury himself in his work. 


**Part 3 - O' Brother**
In 1251, something amazing happened! Terrik was going to be honored in the Grand Republic Ballroom for assisting in cleaning up a foreign drug front for the White Azalea. 

Terrik returned home and visited Otto. It'd been a long time since he'd been home, and it was nice to see Ingrid again. Their comic store was doing pretty good; they had a pretty loyal audience and were carrying some big names. They hadn't spoken all that much since the funeral, so it was pretty awkward. Otto offered to take them to a place him and Ingrid liked. 

They went to a speakeasy in the inner city and talked about their lives. Terrik footed the bill, and was clearly incredibly uncomfortable with the setting. All the talking and dancing and drinking was still scary to him, but he stayed for his brother and sister-in-law. Otto got up to use the bathroom, and Terrik and Ingrid got a chance to talk. Terrik still liked her, but it'd obviously be wrong to talk about that since she's now married. So, they made small chit-chat about the city and their times playing together as kids. They still got along great. 

It'd been a long time since Otto went to the bathroom. Ingrid was worried something was wrong, so Terrik got up to check on him. The bathroom was still locked. He knocked and asked if Otto was ok, but no one answered. Terrik looked over his shoulder, and then jimmied the lock open. Inside, he found the window wide open, and signs of a struggle. Terrik ran outside and into the connecting alleyway. He followed the alleyway and saw two men wailing on Otto. Terrik yelled at ran at them, causing them to flee. Terrik kneeled down to Otto, who'd been stabbed. He was coughing up blood and emitting a low warbling whine. Terrik started hyperventilating and tried treating the wound. He'd lost a lot of blood, and they weren't clean cuts. Otto's voice started to break as he realized he wasn't going to make it. He let out more nonverbal vocalizations as he looked down at the blood streaming from his stomach. Terrik told Otto not to talk, but he said that it was ok, and that he didn't want his last moments to be watching Terrik cry. Terrik tried not to, but he couldn't. Terrik held his hand, and Otto bled out.

Terrik stood up and ran down the alleyway. He found the two muggers attempting to hop a fence a quarter mile down. He pulled them down and disarmed them. He brutally beat them both down, and then killed them with the same knife they'd killed Otto with. As they died, he noticed them both wearing a small white pin of an Azalea. They'd been hired by the White Azalea to kill Terrik's family. 

The police came, and Terrik ran. He took the bodies and drowned them in the river north of Hephaestus. Terrik's story was that he was with Otto when he died, which Ingrid backed up. The killers were never found, so Terrik got away with it. 


**Part 4 - Blackwater**
The grief had hit Terrik hard; he fully blamed himself for his brother's death and had been abusing alcohol and drugs to relieve the grief. Terrik felt like the harder he tried to better himself, the worse he was. 

The day came for Terrik's gala, which felt especially insulting. Terrik tried his best to keep up appearances during the ceremony. During his "acceptance" speech, he mentioned how he felt he didn't deserve any of this, and how much it tore him up inside. He rejected the award due to feeling like he wasn't above anyone else. 

He went to a nearby bar and encountered a drunken Senator Karl Blackwater of Ravmyr. He was a businessman from the east and a prominent member of the EATU. He walked over to Terrik, who wanted to be left alone. He got offended and started listing off his accomplishments. Terrik got up and tried to leave the bar to get away from him, but Blackwater and his gang of cops and elites blocked him from leaving. He called him a mindless killer and a prole. Terrik punched him in the mouth and knocked him down. There, he saw the inside of Blackwater's coat, which happened to be pinned with the emblem of the White Azalea. Terrik was kicked out of the bar.

Terrik knew Blackwater and his friends were in with the Azalea. He stalked Blackwater all the way back to one of his cigar shops in the inner city. After a few hours, Blackwater left, and Terrik snuck in wearing a black mask. He found a hidden wall concealing an assortment of drugs, weapons, and money. Blackwater's shops were drug fronts. Blackwater reentered the shop to grab something he'd forgotten, and caught Terrik. They got into a gunfight and Terrik stole his gun and shot him through the side of the head. He made the kill look like a suicide, and fled the scene. 


**Part 5 - Abelia**
Terrik had killed 3 men in a week. Afterwards, he locked himself in his mansion and nearly drank himself to death. He knew that he was a murderer, but he didn't regret any of it. That lack of remorse sickened him, and caused him to contemplate ending his life. 

Seeking comfort, he went to visit Ingrid and Abelia. Ingrid was still a wreck since what had happened. They'd be fine financially, but she'd have to get a babysitter to watch Abelia while she was working. 

Terrik spoke with Abelia, and tried to comfort her. She'd been having nightmares the past week about her dad. She said that they were gonna get eaten by the "man in the woods." Terrik asked what she meant, and she showed him a drawing. It depicted a tall black silhouette with horns and red eyes holding her hand. She said that she saw the monster in the woods and in her dreams, and that it'd eat the bad people that hurt her dad, and keep her safe while he's away. Terrik sat down besides her and told her that he'd protect her and make sure they get caught. She started crying and gave him a hug. 

Terrik confessed everything he'd been feeling to Ingrid. Ingrid told him that he was a good man who was just dealt a bad hand. It's a hard thing to accept, but right and wrong isn't always black and white. Nobody can be defined by absolute good or absolute bad, especially by other people. The point is: everyone has different standards for right and wrong, and as long as you keep doing your best to do what is right, then you're a good person in her book. 

Terrik thought a lot about what she'd said. All his life he'd been trying to reach impossible standards of moral goodness to prove himself to other people. If what she said was true, than he wasn't a bad person, he was just misguided. If being rich and successful didn't make him feel proud, then what would? The answer came to him almost immediately: avenging Otto. The White Azalea wouldn't rest until him, Ingrid, and Abelia were dead. He couldn't let that happen, and he didn't care if it was illegal, he was going to shut them down. They were impervious to the law anyways, so why couldn't he be too? He wanted justice in the Republic, and he was going to take matters into his own hands. 


**Part 6 - Evan**
It was December, 1251. It'd been 6 months since Otto's death. Terrik stood on his balcony overlooking the city. He breathed in the fresh winter air and felt a surge of pride. In the last 6 months, Terrik had killed hundreds of members of the White Azalea, as well as several corrupt senators and Republic elites. 

In a move that made Abelia supremely uncomfortable, Terrik and Ingrid had begun dating. Terrik didn't care anymore, so he pressured her into being with him. It was a little weird, but it *was* technically a more stable environment for Abelia. She'd been having a nightmares so she was sleeping in the bed with Terrik and Ingrid. 

Karl Blackwater's brother Evan had been following Terrik for some time. He couldn't prove his guilt, but he knew he was responsible for his brother's death. In the middle of the night, he entered Terrik's home and made his way upstairs and into the bedroom. He drew a gun and pointed it at the couple and Abelia. Evan began shaking and crying. He clearly wasn't the murderer type, so Terrik tried to talk him down. 

Evan explained who he was, and what Terrik had done to him. Terrik said he did it in self-defense, and that his brother was working for the Azalea. Evan said that even if that's true, he didn't deserve to die, and it wasn't Terrik's place to make that decision. Terrik said that the Azalea was killing people, that they killed his brother too. If he kills him, then he's no better than them. Evan said that he didn't want to kill Terrik, he wanted to kill someone he loved. Ingrid tried to run, and Terrik tried to attack Evan, but he got several shots out.

Ingrid and Abelia collapsed on the floor. The bullet had pierced Ingrid's side, and two shots hit Abelia in the chest. Terrik took Evan's gun and punched him down. He grabbed them and tried to wake them up, but they were already gone. Evan stood up.

"Now you know what it's like. My brother wasn't a good man, but he didn't deserve this. How does it feel?"

Terrik felt a wave of guilt and rage wash over him. He'd let it happen again. He started shaking and turned around to face Evan. After seeing the raw intensity in his eyes, Evan ran outside and then stopped in a public square. He opened his arms, inviting Terrik to attack him. Terrik tackled him and beat him down. Anyone who tried to intervene, police or civilian, were beaten unconscious. With his final moments, Evan admitted that his plan all along was to make the world see him for who he really was on the inside. Terrik beat him to death and was arrested by Blackwater's corrupt cop friends. 


**Part 7 - Caught**
Terrik was stripped of his titles, publicly shamed, and slammed with a laundry list of phony charges. He wasn't even connected to any of his actual murders, but this was enough to get him the death sentence. He sat in defeat. Evan deserved it. It never mattered what everyone else thought; he was doing the Republic a service. It didn't matter though, it was beyond fixing at this point. And his moral mission got his family killed. 

Senator Kaul personally attended his private execution. He brought an assortment of experimental weapons, and let the executioners torture him with them to test them. Hot oil was poured on his face and ignited, horrifically scarring him. They nearly drowned him over and over. 

They took his eyelids so that he would never be able to rest. His eyes burned horrendously and his vision began to blur. 

They were going to inject him with a lethal dose of some experimental drugs Castle Rock Technology had been working on. He was injected with an extremely dangerous dose of a Redline variant that would've killed him if he hadn't already built up an immunity. 

The extreme emotional distress mixed with the Redline caused him to flood and develop ether capabilities. It gave him the strength to break his binds. He used the hot oil to blind one of the torturers, and he escaped through the sewers. 


**Part 8 - Friend**
Terrik swam across the river during the night and nearly froze to death. Because of this and the repeated attempted drownings, he developed hydrophobia.

He fled into the wastes of Torbekk and was declared dead. His skin turned pale and blue from the cold, and he looked like a walking corpse. Once again, Terrik debated ending his life. He had lost everything and everyone he'd cared about for nothing. He was a horrible person and he felt useless. 

He had ambitions to make things better, but he lacked the power. His funneling enhanced his abilities, but it wasn't enough by itself. He believes man is inherently evil, and there's no way to fix or undo that. His mission to fix the Republic was doomed from the start. What is "right" is relative to the situation and no one really cares.

He made camp in a cave, and was very nearly dead when he heard a voice calling him to explore deeper. He got up and stumbled deeper and deeper into the cave. The floor collapsed and he fell deep into the earth. He used his ether to cushion his fall, and fall on his chin. 

Looking forward, he saw something he never would've expected. Malfaust; the Ancient Red Dragon. Terrik shakily got to his feet and prepared to fight the beast. Malfaust was surprisingly civil, and offered him food and drink. Terrik refused by principle, but was too weak to fight well. Malfaust saw strength in him unlike anyone else in Serendipita. His willpower and determination to make the world a better place may have been born of self-hatred, but it made him a force to be reckoned with. Terrik was flattered, and with few other options, he took the food and began eating. 

Malfaust made him an offer. They both wanted the same thing: to make the world a better place. They agreed that mankind couldn't effectively rule itself. In fact, it wasn't meant to. This world was born into Draconic rule. The only reason it fell apart was because man believed they deserved rulership for themselves. They wiped out his entire species and took everything from him. Life was better back then, and it could be again. Together, they could bring about a world free of the Republic, free of corruption, free of manmade fallacies of right and wrong. Terrik was special, and together they could bring about real change. 

Terrik was skeptical, but Malfaust didn't rush him. He showed him what life could be like under his rule, and earned his trust. Terrik saw a world free from the pain he'd experienced, and a way to do work he knew he could feel proud of. Malfaust believed he was doing the right thing, he just needed direction and a plan. Terrik agreed, but he couldn't return to Arcohaem in his current state. 

Terrik wanted to become a symbol of justice and vengeance; he wanted to become the Republic's worst nightmare. He thought back to Abelia's drawing; he would become a monster, and an envoy of the highest order. As Malfaust's right hand man, they would bring about the second coming of the Draconic Era. 

Malfaust designed his suit after Abelia's drawing. He was tall, slender, and looked impossibly black. Malfaust imbued the suit with Draconic technology and ether to prevent him from dying. His skin was sewn back together where it'd split, his infections were treated, the exoskeleton accounted for his damaged body, and mechanical implants prevented him from going blind and made his eyes glow a deep fiery red. 

The Industrial Revolution was kicking off the broader Serendipita, and Malfaust had a plan...
