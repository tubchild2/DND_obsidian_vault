### Overview
Founded 12c 604
At war with [[Frigoshia]] since 1258
Progressive, industrial, and corrupt

**Central Tribes**
	The wealthy urban and suburban sectors of the Republic
	Controls most production, trade, military, and government
	Filled with factories and industrial smog
	Responsible for the creation of the Republic
	Where the Senate meets
	Full of rich, materialistic snobs, and the military

**East Tribes (EATU)**
	Part of the Republic
	Allied into the [[East Arcohaem Trade Union]]
	Has their own senate that decides what the senators should collectively vote for in Republic Senate hearings
	Controls Lemnaeus, which is responsible for nearly half of all Republic trade
	Pretty sensible, but still materialistic and over industrialized
	Has a lot of immigrants and foreign trade

**South Tribes**
	Part of the Republic
	Uninterested / flippant in politics
	Vote to preserve their own independence
	Similar culture to [[Frigoshia]], but they don't want to go to war
	They allow the Republic given the Republic allows their freedoms
	Tribal, low population density, and largely uninterested in politics
	They don't care

### Government
#### Law
**How the Senate Works**
- Each tribe elects 5 senators (typically through voting, inheritance, or by a local leader). 
- Senators appoint advisors, aides, and delegates. 
	- Advisors help them to research and make decisions
	- Aides provide practical assistance
	- Delegates represent them in the senate when they're unavailable
- Senate hearings happen 3 times a week in Hephaestus, where they make decisions regarding law, leadership, and the military.
- The Chancellor of the Senate is elected every 5 years. He can vote, and manages the hearings. 
- Senators must have 5 years of Senate experience before they can be elected Chancellor. 
- The Lord Commander is chosen by the Chancellor when one dies or retires. They oversee the Royal Guard. 
- The Royal Guard is chosen through application and review by the Chancellor and Lord Commander. 
- Privates are inducted by recruiters --> majors --> captains --> commanders --> generals

**Law Enforcement**
- Republic law is created in the Senate, tribal law is created in tribal court by the Governor / by tribal leaders
- Local police / champions --> Tribal police / warchiefs --> Royal Guard --> Military Intervention
- Local court / chieftain --> tribal court / tribal leader --> The Senate
- Local prison --> tribal prison --> Ullr

**Central and Eastern Law**
- Governors are elected by majority vote every 4 years, granted they have at least 3 years of experience as a mayor. 
- Mayors are elected by majority vote every 3 years
- Policemen apply for the job and the best applicant is selected
- Local judges apply for the job and the best applicant is selected
- Tribal judges apply for the job and the best applicant is selected, granted they have at least 3 years of experience as a local judge. 

**EATU Law**
- The President of the EATU is elected every 4 years
- The President has a seat in the senate

**Southern Law**
- Tribal leaders inherit their power. If no heirs exist, one of their chieftains ascend the role. 
- Senators are chosen by tribal leaders. 
- Chieftains inherit their power. If no heirs exist, the region the tribe oversees elects a new chieftain. 
- Champions are chosen by local chieftains.

### Politics / The Corruption
The Good
- [[Finn|Senator Finn]] is a goodhearted diplomat.
- [[Senator Hayes]]. Is an incredibly good person, but he's an idiot. Genuine third-grade level education at best. However, he has a heart of solid 24 karat gold. 
- [[Kragknull|Commander Kragknull]] is a firm but wise man. 
- Senator Alis Venn. Sees things going downhill. Doesn't want to be involved in the war at all. Would like to make things better, but is too scared. From the east. 
- [[Thane|President Thane]]. A devout [[Normons|Normon]], meaning he refuses to do things that conflict with his morality. 

The Neutral
- [[House Havran]]. Strict, a little verbally abusive, and has dipped their toes into criminal activity for selfish reasons. They aren't running any criminal organizations, but they're no saints.  
- [[Cedtris Coparol]]. Being groomed for the position of / is the replacement for Estis Macarra as overseer of the Republic bank once the assassination goes through. Young, inexperienced, naive, and easily persuaded by [[Kaul|Chancellor Kaul]]. In his early twenties.
- Senator Cala Mireth. A selfish and disinterested woman. Only cares about keeping his southern tribe out of the war, and out of as many government sponsored programs as possible. Strategically detached at best.
- Darian Voss. His family runs Lemnaeus. He's filthy rich and wants more trade with the rest of [[Serendipita]] at all costs.
- Senator Elric. Was personally wronged by the corruption. Tied up in criminal dealings due to owing a debt to them. Wants things to get better, but doesn't have a choice anymore. Feels powerless.
- Mr. F. A criminal for hire. Has killed, beaten, stolen, and blackmailed people. Inspired by Mike Ehrmantraut: morally neutral. Does what he's hired to do. Thinks law-breaking is ethically relative. Has lines he won't cross, like anything involving children or innocent people. He doesn't do anything for free, and he doesn't ask questions.
- The South doesn't care about anything that's going on and just wants to preserve their interests. They will vote on whatever they want and fain interest if it means getting people to vote for what they want. They can be bought very easily, but they don't swing on southern matters. They don't even have advisors because they know exactly what they want.

The hubs are well hidden, constantly moving, and protected by armed soldiers with access to [[Serendipita]]'s nastiest [[Serendipita|E-Tech]]. Deals are planned in advance, there's no browsing, and things move quickly. They themselves aren't an organization, but are rather a community or smaller organizations. Majority of [[Arcohaem]]'s elite on both sides deal with the hollow.

	- They represent a collective of pirates, thieves, bounty hunters, assassins, hitmen, corrupt lawyers, corrupt policemen, corrupt guards, spies, and illegal traders.  
- [[Kaul|Chancellor Kaul]] is double dealing and committing massive amounts of treason with [[Atka|King Atka]], [[Malfaust]], and [[Terrik|the Wraith]].
- Senator B. Current CEO of [[Castle Rock Technology]]. Has been using the war to sell weapons, and taking designs from [[Terrik|the Wraith]] via [[Kaul]].
