#### Act 0
World
- Arcohaem is the arctic region of Serendipita
- The Republic of Arcohaem forced industrialization when the north didn't want to change
- The north receded from the Republic and formed the Kingdom of Frigoshia
- Civil war has been destroying Arcohaem for over a decade, especially on the Howling Front
- Frigoshia has better warriors, but the Republic has better weapons

PCs
- Elite strike team of Republic soldiers
- Reputation for being inconsistent and unpredictable
- Partially made up of criminals
- Called the Wolves of Arcohaem
- One of them was traumatized by the Wraith

**Chapter 3: Your Mission**
- They meet Kragknull in the senate building in Hephaestus

- Some Republic soldiers got lost and discovered a Frigoshian transport carrying dragon incubation equipment
- Frigoshia is incubating dragons, which could mean the end of the Republic, of Arcohaem, and of Serendipita
- The soldiers reported their discovery to the Senate, who proposed a mission: a strike team will infiltrate the Frigoshian capital of Torhild, hunt down the program, and destroy any draconic life found there before it's too late.

###### Part 2: Just Like the Simulations
Who are they fighting?

**Chapter 4: Ammalor**
- They travel from Hephaestus into the Republic city of Ammalor, which is known for its trade and artisans
- Kragknull tests their new long-range communicators, and checks in on them. He's hanging out with Senator Finn. 
- They find a sketchy man named Marrek Renn to take them to Freyrhage. He doubles the price due to "volcanic activity" in the northern waters, and due to the dangers of sailing into enemy territory. He has a strict no questions asked policy, and it extends both ways. 
- On the ship, some unsettling noises and bubbling emanate from the waters. Something falls into the water on the back end of the ship, and the bubbling stops. Marrek claims it's the "volcanic activity" he warned about earlier. 
- The Captain owns a dagger from pre-122, which is impossibly old. The tribes of Arcohaem weren't even founded then, but it's fairly advanced. So, who made it? It has draconic emblems and a red triangle carved into it. The thought that there's always something out there for him to discover keeps him sailing. 

**Chapter 5: Freyrhage**
- They dock at Freyrhage, which recently declared neutrality and left Frigoshia. To protect themselves, the town hired a private army of mercenaries and fighters. 
- The people are sweet and normal, but they're worried about if they're going to survive. Frigoshia is definitely going to try and reconquer them, and the attack could happen any day. They're trying to arm themselves and fortify their village. 
- They meet the Perssons. 
	- The mother, Emily, is stressed about her family and has been working herself to death to try and get a way out of Freyrhage. She doesn't know if the militia will be enough or not. 
	- The father, Erik, is a stoic man who's all but given up, much to the dismay of Emily. He has faith in the private army. He speaks in grunts, sits in his underwear, and enjoys eating and drinking. 
	- The son, Danny, is an incredibly nervous and shy kid. He's scared to talk to anyone or to do anything that might be against the rules. He's worried about his family. 
	- The daughter, Danielle, is extremely vibrant and outgoing. She's too young to understand the situation, so she's blissfully oblivious. She's incredibly sweet. 
	- The grandmother, Annette, is kind of flirtatious and makes a lot of jokes. She's wheelchair bound. 
- The Perssons invite them over for roast duck. 
	- Danielle gives one of them her pop-up book, which has her name and address written on the inside of it. 
	- Emily has started deadbolting all their windows and doors at night. They want to leave, but it's too expensive and they don't know if Annette will survive the journey. 
	- Danny is awestruck by their war stories.
	- Erik eats the food with gusto and appreciates anyone else who does too. 
	- Annette picks one Wolf and flirts with them. 
	- Eventually, Emily puts on some music and they dance and drink. 

**Chapter 6: Borderlands**
- They leave the town of Freyrhage and continue moving west
- About 6 miles from town, they see a black silhouette atop a far away cliff. It almost looks human. Looking closer, it's gone. 
- They spot a Frigoshian battalion in a distant valley preparing to invade Freyrhage. 100-200 soldiers minimum. There are crates loaded with explosives and weapons. The militia stands no chance.

- He has photos to prove he has a wife and kids. He says he's a scout and that he isn't important. 
- His body language is very relaxed, he makes jokes, he never yells, he makes eye contact, and he has a lot of physicality to him. He points and whips his neck around. He's got that dead-eyed Frigoshian look, so no matter what he does, his eyes never change. 
- He asks them questions about who they are and what they're doing. He reveals what they're planning in exchange for his life; at sundown tonight, they're going to destroy Freyrhage. While he's leaving, he swipes the pop-up book, since it looks odd to them. 

**Chapter 7: Me? I'm Nobody**

That means he isn't a scout, they have no clue who he was or what he wanted, and he now knows about the Perssons. He isn't dumb enough to attack a strike team of soldiers by himself. Something's wrong.
- They rush back to Freyrhage as the sun is setting. The front guards are missing. They were killed, and they had their eyelids taken so that "they couldn't rest." 
- The Persson's door is wide open, and their window is smashed. The living room is trashed, the chairs are knocked over, Annette is dead, and there's a long red bloodstain across the carpet. Candlelight is coming from the kitchen door. 
- Erik and Emily are tied to chairs and gagged, and the kids are tied to each other in the corner.

- He's the captain of the Frigoshian battalion. He recognized them and wondered what they were doing in Freyrhage of all places.
