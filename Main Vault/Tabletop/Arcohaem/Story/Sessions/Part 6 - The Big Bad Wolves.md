#### Into Torhild
The Wraith
- Once they wake up, they hear it. It's on them again. 

Into Frigoshia
- The citizens are starving, angry, and terrified. They're good people. 
- There's a sharp class divide. The government is fat, happy, and the ones actually carrying the hatred. 

Rorik and Rosie
- Her ex-husband was in the Republic military, but he defected so that they could be together.
- He faked his death and moved to Torhild
- She believes in the value of their culture even if her husband didn't.
- Good, smart, and fair -- like Howard Hamlin
- She's instantly suspicious of them and thinks they're Republic soldiers
- She'll stick up for them and argue her beliefs
- She's completely radicalized
- She's been protecting Rorik Valgok from being captured and trying to rehabilitate him
	- He's really missed them
	- It's been almost a year since he went missing
	- He was capture and brought to a prison in Arrah for several months
	- He befriended someone named 'V,' and he let him go free right before the town was destroyed by something incredibly powerful
	- He lived in the woods for a little bit
	- He snuck into Torhild via the graveyard and got caught by Rosie, who was visiting her husband
	- She gave him food, water, and a place to stay, temporarily. It's been a month since then.
	- He's been working on an escape plan. 

The Wraith
- It'll come for them the following night. It doesn't like to be seen, and it's afraid of water. 
- They hear him before they see him, once they're alone. 
- Dead silence as they have a chance to get away from windows, barricade the room, and hide.
- They feel a primal fear, like a child being afraid of the dark, or afraid of the outside world, or a fear that someone is going to break in. You know he's here, but you don't know where. 
- He'll get Ron alone and seal the door behind them.
	- He mentions how he thought his apology was irritating and out of touch.   
	- He was prevented from killing them by his boss. 
	- He'll offer him a chance to leave by himself and survive. 
	- He'll leave to let him think about it if Ron doesn't escalate the situation. 
	- They can't get into the room unless they do a lot of damage to the door.
