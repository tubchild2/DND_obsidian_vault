**WHERE RORIK WAS** (optional reading)
A little over a year ago, the Wolves of Arcohaem were fighting around Beirdall. During the fight, Rorik was hit by a shell and fell into a 50 foot ravine, where he was presumed dead. The thick fog made it impossible to confirm his death. The Wolves were then flanked by enemy forces, forcing them to retreat prematurely. 

Rorik barely survived and spent the next 12 hours attempting to repair his broken body. Unfortunately, he was discovered by the Frigoshians before he was able to get very far. Despite his best efforts, they knocked him unconscious and restrained him. He was placed into the back of a wagon and taken north. Rorik was given limited food and water and left almost entirely exposed to the elements. 

After 6 days of travel, a guard entered the wagon and knocked him unconscious. When he woke up, he was in a holding cell in the town of Arrah. His arms and feet were shackled, he was guarded by two soldiers, and he would be injected with depressants to inhibit his cognitive functions, including ether. The room had a small barred window, a pit toilet, a wafer-thin sleeping pad, and a single locked door. Though muffled, Rorik could make out a conversation between two men on the opposite side of the door. They were originally going to kill him, but they received orders to send him somewhere confidential; neither of them knows where, so they're waiting for instructions to arrive. The rest of it was too muffled to make out. 

A masked guard named 'V' entered the cell. He was going to be watching over Rorik until they were ready to relocate him. Rorik would be fed and given water twice a day. Additionally, each morning and evening his cell would be searched, and he'd be given the inhibitors. At the first sign of defiance, they'd take his blankets and clothes and beat him. This routine would repeat indefinitely. It couldn't be that long, could it?

 \- 9 months later -

V was a reclusive and opinionated man. He didn't want to speak with Rorik, and he didn't want to still be in Arrah. He had a family back in Torhild. It was tough being away from them for so long. Rorik had opened up about his wife, Eleanor, which softened V's disposition a little. V would occasionally speak about having old friends in the Republic he couldn't see anymore. He'd visited Sindri many times when he was a young man, meaning he and Rorik may have met each other at one point. Rorik wondered if he'd recognize him, but V refused to take off his mask. It's funny how your perception of someone can change how you picture them. 

It'd been a horrible 9 months. The depressants had worsened his disposition and will to resist, making the whole experience that much more defeating. V slipped into his cell and placed a bowl of soup into his shackled hands. They'd received confidential orders on where to take Rorik. He was going to be moved in the morning. V's voice trembled a little. They didn't know where Rorik was going to be taken, but they both knew it was bad. 

Rorik couldn't sleep. He tossed and turned and eventually just stared up at the ceiling. He missed his friends, he missed his wife, and he missed cheese. He turned to face the door and heard it unlock. Rorik sat up, and his eyes widened. He shuffled over to the door, and to his shock, it cracked open. Was this a miracle? Peering out the door, he didn't see the guards anywhere. Maybe it was a miracle!

Rorik could feel his heart racing. His powers were still inhibited, but they'd be back by morning. He shuffled out of the building and saw two guards on patrol nearby. He crept across the narrow snowy path and into a wooden shack. He scoured the tools inside and found a hacksaw. Bracing it with his feet, he frantically ran the length of the blade back and forth across the chains binding his hands. After weakening them, he was able to wrap them around a post in the room and snap them apart. He grasped the saw and began to hack at the chains binding his legs. After some effort, they snapped apart too. He peered out the door and saw more guards coming his way. 

Rorik had a few options on where to go next. The east and west were blocked by a cradle of mountains. If he went north, he'd likely freeze to death in the tundra before he made it to the water or to another town. If he went south, he'd run into the woods, where he knew he could at least find food and make shelter. His training prepared him for situations exactly like these. He waited for a break in the guards' attention and barreled into the woods. 

He ran for hours until finally reaching a small clearing and an abandoned cabin. Nearby was a stream of fresh water. He collapsed inside and began to shake with joy. Over the coming days, his powers slowly returned, allowing him to break off his shackles, begin catching fish, and work on repairing the cabin. He was planning on storing a bunch of food and then flying back home, but something was wrong. Every time he'd attempt to fly, teleport, or move faster, his ether would explode in a mist of red gas. Either he hadn't fully regained his power, or something nearby was overriding his abilities. If he wanted to escape, he'd have to flee on foot and sneak through enemy territory, something that's nigh impossible to do without his strength back and more resources. 

Over the next few months, he slowly regained his strength and began formulating an escape plan. He lived in Thrall, and would occasionally sneak into Torhild to get supplies. Soon, he'll be able to get out of here...
