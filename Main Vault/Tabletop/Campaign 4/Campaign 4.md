##### Worldbuilding
**Methodology**
- Automate everything you can for speed, scope, detail, and accuracy
- Basic plan
	- World Bible
		- Concrete and immutable source
		- Used as the ultimate source for everything else
		- Tracks all coming data
	- Geography
		- Mountains
		- Land / water
		- Map features
	- Factions
		- Use sociology course to design societies and their cultures better 
	- Timeline
		- Split into definitive and realistic eras and suberas
		- Create a foundation that other events can build off of later
	- Landmarks
		- Villages, cities, ruins
- Characters need more identity and personality, especially important ones. They need to be figured out and rehearsed earlier so that they don't end up falling flat when the time comes. 
- Use Atlas VTT


##### Narrative
**Methodology**
- Plot the campaign out in a little bit more detail. Each session should serve a purpose, have a source of fun/intrigue, and feel self-contained. 
- While making each session carry one major narrative goal is efficient, it isn't very practical. The campaign should've been 12 arcs, not 12 sessions.
- I feel that with Arcohaem, not enough room was left for player choice. It was very railroaded because I had a story I wanted to tell and didn't want to deviate from it. 

**Traits**
- Grand scope
- World spanning adventure
- Intricately designed
- Longer runtime (multi-arc)
- Ticking clock
- Zero-to-hero. Built in power fantasy. 

**PCs**
- Give players choices on how to progress at the end of each session
- Pay more attention to individual player stories
- Let players design their team together during a designated session 0


##### Gameplay
**Session Preparation**
- It's helpful to have a process for developing each session. That goes hand-in-hand with the amount of time you spent laying out each session ahead of time. The more narrative work you do on the campaign's plot ahead of time, the easier and faster prep will be when the time comes for each session. 

**Ruleset**
- Custom TTRPG
- DND 5.5e as basis
- Customized classes, backgrounds, and species(?)
- Turn / spotlight based combat
- Daggerheart inspiration for narrative focus
- Start at level 1
