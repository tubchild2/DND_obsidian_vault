### Chapter 9: Rain Fire!
**Introduction**
It's been a couple of days since Freyrhage. You moved dead west towards the [[Frigoshia|Frigoshian]] city of Beirdall with the plan of entering the capital from the northern mountains. With some basic stealth, it should be pretty easy to get in and cross the river into Aburbekk and get out of the city. 

You're in the wilderness. It's been a stressful couple of days. 
*What do you do?*

**Moving West**
As you crest the hill overlooking Beirdall, you begin to see smoke, and hear explosions in the distance. Several Republic gunships have moved up the river and have begun an all out siege in the center of the city. Mortars from both sides are raining fire on the entire city, taking flight off the table. 

Your goal is to get across the river, and get as far north west as possible. The west end of the city is going to be crawling with [[Frigoshia|Frigoshians]], but the siege means the central river is also crawling with allies. 
*What do you do?*

**Entering the City**
It's completely war-torn. The structures are collapsed in on themselves and turned to an ashy white. Piles of destroyed concrete line the streets, as well as the remains of what was once the citizens of Beirdall. It's eerily quiet, other than the distant sound of battle coming from the west. 
In that eerie, quiet stillness, you hear a few rocks slip, and the sound of footsteps coming from a building near you.
*What do you do?*

(investigation)
	You enter the collapsed building. It appears to have been a home of some kind at one point. There are family photos, what looks to be the remnants of a dining room table and a couch, and there's a gigantic scorch mark in the center of the room where something likely detonated. Poorly hidden under a concrete covered desk is a young boy. 

(interacting with him)
- He's terrified of you. 
- His home was hit by a mortar a few days ago (unclear which side)
- He needs to get to his family in Torhild, but he can't get help from anyone or try to cross the river since he's scared to go near the fighting. He also can't travel alone since he'll starve, dehydrate, or freeze to death. 
*What do you do?*
	They will likely decide to try and help the boy. If they don't, then they're just straight up evil atp.

**Approaching the Central River**
They encounter the [[The Karlstad Crusaders|Karlstad Crusaders]], who are here helping with the Republic's mission to conquer the city. 


### Chapter 10: Shoreline Siege
The party, [[The Karlstad Crusaders]], and the orphan cross the river and enter the cramped and destroyed West End. There, they encounter several [[Frigoshia|Frigoshian]] soldiers. 

Attempt to separate [[The Karlstad Crusaders]] as soon as possible to prevent initiative bloat. Keep the [[Frigoshia|Frigoshian]] soldier's turns simple. Greatly buff their attacks as well. 

The orphan gets scared and start running out of cover. He's greeted by a [[Frigoshia|Frigoshian]] captain. He speaks kindly and reassures him. He sees that he's scared, so he wipes off a tear with his leather glove. He pulls out his pistol and gives it to the child, and starts talking to him about what it's used for. He then finds out they've been with a Republic strike team, and his demeaner instantly shifts to disappointment, and the party has an opportunity to intervene. If they get noticed, he shoots the kid. If they don't do anything, he shoots the kid. If they execute a quick and stealthy plan, they can save him, but he'll still try to shoot the kid (he'll likely miss or simply wound him).
