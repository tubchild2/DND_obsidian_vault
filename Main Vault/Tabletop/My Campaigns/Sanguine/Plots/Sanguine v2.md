### ACT 1
**Chapter 1: Origin**
We see Melvin Delaney, a 9 year old nobody and scoundrel born in a lower class mining town. His father is a cowardly and kind man. He had an impeccable moral compass, but his lack of will to defend himself was frustrating. His mother was a fiery law-breaker who regularly got into trouble with the law. Melvin was overall a sweet kid, who also got in his fair share of trouble. 

One day, Melvin gets caught trying to steal some food from a baker for his hungry friend who's parents weren't getting paid enough because the Culling stole all the [[Sanguinine]] they mined and were going to sell. In the process of escaping, he demonstrates the signs of [[Ferromancy]]. The baker reports him to the Culling out of spite. 

Melvin gets scolded by his parents, and his mother recites something I haven't figured out yet. Basically, it's about how he should always do what is right, even if it sucks. Something along the lines of with great power comes great responsibility, and how being a bad person pays off and will make you successful, but it won't make you happy. 

A pair of inquisitors arrive at his home. After being interrogated and deciding not to go with them, they attack him and ram a spike through his chest. His mother attacks the inquisitors and is promptly killed. They leave, reasonably assuming him dead. His cowardly father is spared but warned not to do anything, which is accepts. The local doctor is able to secretly save Mell, but he's permanently weakened by the attack. 



**Chapter 2: A Dark Path**
Melvin grows up in hiding with his father. Melvin changes his name each time he risks being caught, eventually landing on Mell Doyle. After his father's death, he joins a gang of outlaws. He rejects joining the rebellion despite his hatred for the Culling since he's making good money and living a comfortable life. His inherent good nature shines through periodically, but he's gone down a dark path. 

Mell has good [[Ferromancy|Ferromantic]] potential, but he doesn't want to use it, since his worst nightmare is to attract the attention of the inquisitors, who believe him to be dead.

His gang rob a caravan on horseback, and Mell is called a monster by the victim. He remembers his mother screaming that at the inquisitor, so he feels a little hurt. He leaves them without returning anything anyways. One of the items he stole was a broken pocket mirror. He feels frustrated when he gazes in it, and so, in a tiny act of rebellion, Mell uses his abilities in secret to lift the mirror. 



**Chapter 3: Close Encounters**
Mell is still feeling frustrated, and proposes robbing someone who isn't innocent. His gang understands the compulsion, but they live in desperate times. You can be an honorable thief, and that's ok, but waiting around for the perfect Robin Hood job to fall into their laps is going to take too long, and more importantly, likely attract the attention of people they don't want to deal with. That kind of thinking is dangerous. 

Mell agrees with them and they proceed with a heist on a rebellion hideout in a town. Once they arrive, they discover a pair of Inquisitors in the area that got the location of the hideout from the same source as them. Mell and his gang prepare to flee, but then he sees a suspicious rebel about to be caught by an inquisitor. An urge to do something good pushes him try to deescalate by lying to the Inquisitor about the rebel. His gang abandons him; Mell somewhat expected as much. 

His lies work at first, but get caught when another Inquisitor intervenes. Mell lies to get them a head start, and then they flee through the town. Mell feels like an idiot for doing this, but is in too deep now. Mel is forced to use his powers to fully escape, which puts him on the radar of the Culling. 



**Chapter 4: On the Run**
The rebel's name is Clifford "Cliff" Hall. He's 5 years younger than Mell, and fresh to the whole disobeying the law thing. He's naive, inexperienced, and unskilled, but he has an impeccable moral compass and is a kind and respectable dude. He carries a [[Sanguinine]] revolver, but won't use it unless he absolutely has to. He's no older than 20.

Mell wants to change his identity, and help Cliff to do the same and to get to safety. Cliff pushes him about using his [[Ferromancy]] for good and joining the rebellion, but Mell strongly resists. Saving Cliff will cost him greatly, since he no longer has a gang or a usable identity. Cliff argues that if they go to the rebellion home base, Mell can guarantee Cliff's safety, and get paid back for his losses. 

Mell wants the money from the Rebellion, but in order to even get to home base, they'd need to take the train. Doing so requires money they don't have and interaction with the public that they don't want. The rebellion is a lost cause anyways. Mell will help Cliff until he's certain he's safe. Cliff begrudgingly obliges. 

Mell continues hiding his abilities, but Cliff keeps insisting that their life would be way easier if he'd use them, but not obviously. Mell says no at first, but starts doing so because it *is* so much easier to get around and do things.

Mell meets up with a sketchy dude he gets fake IDs from. Him and Cliff get some, and then are betrayed by the dealer and reported to the Inquisition for a paycheck. To escape, Mell pushes Cliff to shoot a man. 

Once out of town, Mell sees Cliff sitting alone looking out on the sunset. Cliff is dealing with feelings of major guilt. Mell tells him that he had to do it, and that it was for the greater good. He quotes his old gang from before, and explains that waiting around for the right thing will get you killed. He cites the consequences they're both facing at the moment for their "good" actions. Cliff yells for the first time that he doesn't like Mell, and he doesn't care if doing "good" pays or not. Cliff nearly killed a man and can't live a life like that. Cliff knows Mell feels some modicum of guilt for the things he's done, but he's too materialistic to deal with it. Mell says he doesn't feel guilty since he had to do what he did to survive. Cliff says he didn't *have* to do anything, he's just scared of his conscience. Mell tells Cliff to fend for himself and kocks over his bag. The pocket mirror falls out, and Mell picks it up. At that moment, it clicks that he isn't happy with the life he's living, but he's too prideful to admit it in the moment. 



**Chapter 5: Trial**
On his own again, Mell is wracked with guilt. He knows Cliff is going to get himself killed fighting his moral mission, and he can't decide if he's an idiot or a genius. Whatever he is, he's risking his life unnecessarily. 

Mell has been practicing [[Ferromancy]], and is getting fairly good with it. He's a novice now. He breaks apart his pistol for a node of [[Sanguinine]] to use in an emergency. 

Mell goes into a town and gets work as a bounty hunter, trying to move on from the whole situation. The suspiciously well paying mission ends violently when a rival gang goes after the quarry and knocks them unconscious. Mell kidnaps bagged quarry and kills / wounds multiple people to get it. He uses the [[Sanguinine]] to shoot plasma blasts and rip open metal doors. 

As it turns out, the quarry is a young kid, no older than 11. The boy is scared, and Mell questions why he's even worth so much. As it turns out, the kid is also a [[Ferromancy|Ferromancer]], but his potential is *tiny*. He would never under any circumstances pose any real threat to the Culling, but the client wants to sell him for money. The boy's name is Spencer "Spenny" Campbell. Mell didn't know he was a child, and is definitely not going to sell him to the inquisition. 



**Chapter 6: Acceptance**
Spenny is obnoxious as hell, but he's a sweet kid who isn't trying to annoy anyone. Mell yells at him to shut up, and feels bad so he tries to be more patient and considerate. Mell realizes he can't take care of this kid. His parents are long since dead, and giving him to any living relatives would buy him no more than a month. 

Mell admits to himself that he was wrong, and goes after Cliff to get the location of the Rebel hideout. He gives Spenny a [[Sanguinine]] pistol to protect himself, since Cliff is being hunted by the inquisition, and things are going to get dangerous. Mell finds Cliff exactly where he said he'd be, trying to hop on the next train north. 

They make up, and get on the train together. Two inquisitors catch up to them on horseback as the train is leaving, which Mell complains about how that's even possible. Mell, Cliff, and Spenny begin moving through the train, attempting to get away from the now pursuing inquisitors. Cliff protects Spenny and fires at the Inquisitors, something he has little problem doing. Mell uses his powers to try and slow them down and knock them off the train. His wits and creativity give him an edge in the fight, but he lacks the raw power to engage physically. The other passengers evade them, and Mell has to protect them from the inquisitor, who's using them as bait for Mell. Mell tricks one inquisitor off the train and steals his needle. The other is smarter and forces Mell into sword combat, something he has little to no experience in and is worse at due to his weakened arm from his childhood wound. The Inquisitor goes after Spenny after getting past Mell, forcing a negotiation. Cliff shoots the Inquisitor, who redirects the blast and burns Spenny's arm, knocking him to the ground. Mell uses the opportunity to attempt to physically restrain the inquisitor. It fails, but with help from Cliff, they're able to detach the train car and lose the Inquisitor. That's what they think, but then in an insane display of strength, the inquisitor leaps from the detached train car towards their car. Mell uses his node of [[Sanguinine]] and blasts the inquisitor in the chest with plasma before he can land, letting them barely get away. 



### ACT 2
They make it to the rebellion home base and Spenny is cared for. Cliff guides Mell to the leader of the rebellion, and Mell is hesitant about joining still. He still thinks it's impossible to kill the Needle King. After running some tests on him, they find out that Mell has an insanely high [[Ferromancy|Ferromantic]] potential, one capable of rivalling the Needle King if carefully attended to. 

They have two plans for defeating the Needle King. The primary plan is to cut off the Needle King's supply lines and weaken his control over time, and then eventually lure him out of his fortress and trap and kill him. The secondary plan is Mell; if Mell were to complete his training, he could kill the Needle King himself. 

This act is made up of multiple small missions as Mell grows in power, and becomes overconfident. He starts matching inquisitors in power. He still thinks the rebellion is an exercise in futility, but it's enough to make him feel good about himself while still keeping him alive. His power starts to become so great that his old tendencies begin to creep in. He begins to wonder what kind of an outlaw he could be now, with his powers. Inquisitors aren't even that big of a threat anymore; he could use his powers in broad daylight. He feels like he deserves more, and is destined for more than these small missions. The rebellion could be so much greater if they just abandoned some of their codes. 

Cliff continuously warns him against that kind of thinking. Sure he could get way more money as an outlaw, and he could probably help the rebellion more if he violated their rules, but it isn't the right way to do things. Mell thinks that's nothing but platitudes, and thinks he can be a morally grey hero. He starts stealing and breaking all of their rules. 

Mell goes against his orders on a mission with Cliff. This mission involves baiting the Needle King out of his fortress, and is what everything has built up to. Mell isn't taking any chances at it failing. Cliff tells him no, but Mell ignores the plan anyways, since he's sure he can protect them both just fine and do things better. Unfortunately, what Mell didn't except is the Needle King to anticipate their mission, ignore their bait, ambush them, and personally go after Mell. The Needle King decapitates Cliff, and butchers about 30 other rebels. Mell wrathfully attacks him, and gets his arm cut off. Mell tries to run, but is woefully outmatched. Mell is beaten and stabbed and sliced. Mell falls into a river and nearly drowns swimming out of the compound they were raiding. 



### ACT 3
Mell once again questions if doing the right thing is even worth it anymore. It'd been a few months. The rebellion is crippled, he's missing an arm, and his best friend is dead. He steals a prosthetic [[Sanguinine]] operated arm, and fully masters [[Ferromancy]]. He's being discreet and only using his power for good, so evading the inquisitors isn't too big of a deal. 

He reunites with the rebellion, who are extremely upset with him. With patience, he manages to rejoin, but they don't trust him nearly as much. Their resources are dwindling, and their ranks are low. They have one plan, which is a full assault on the Needle King's fortress, and the detonation of the [[Sanguinine]] hoard he sits on. They don't really have any more options, since the Culling has tightened its grip and made it much harder for them to attempt any of their usual missions. The plan isn't very popular. 

Mell debates whether he should endorse this plan or not. The rebellion is dying, and this isn't a very good plan. There's very little strategy to it, it's just a Hail Mary / brute force with everything they have left. Mell doesn't know if he can take the Needle King in a fight, since last time it went terribly. 

Mell knows that this mission is suicide, but he doesn't know if he's ready to die for a cause. Mell thinks about Cliff, who, down to the very end, believed in fighting for something beyond yourself. Everyone involved in this mission would be making sacrifices, almost all of them would lose their lives. But Mell knows, if he didn't do this, if he let them die, if he let the Culling keep winning, he would always regret it. He'd spent his life being selfish and hurting people, and it brought him nothing. He'd do it. 

Mell accepts the mission. 

Everything goes according to plan, and they push through the impact crater towards the fortress. The Needle King's chamber is the only thing standing between Mell and detonating the [[Sanguinine]] hoard. The two begin to duel, and at first, Mell is losing horribly. The Needle King taunts him and questions why someone of his power wouldn't just join them and would instead get himself killed. He used to be just like Mell, but he used his powers "correctly" and earned "real success." His advanced exosuit grants him ridiculous power, and lets him wail on Mell over and over, and constantly keep him on the run. The rest of the rebellion detonates the smaller hoards throughout the compound, causing the building to begin collapsing. The fight drags on through the collapse, and Mell keeps getting closer and closer. At the very last moment, the Needle King pounces and crushes his prosthetic arm. He again begs Mell to stop this and join him, since together no one would ever be able to overthrow their rule. He offers Mell money, power, castles: whatever Mell wants he can have. If Mell succeeds, not only will he likely kill himself, but he'll destroy the single most powerful government the world has ever seen and lose the chance of ever having that much wealth ever again. It's stupid! 

Mell agrees that it's stupid. From every logical standpoint, his choice to resist and give up everything he wants it stupid. Doing the right thing is awful and hard, but it allows you to die with pride. And he'll be damned if he ever ignores that voice in his head that tells him what's right and what's wrong ever again. He calls Mell a fool and tells him that pride or not he's still going to die. He lifts his needle to stab him. Just then, Mell sees his own needle on the ground behind the King. He lifts it and rams it through both of them, fatally wounding himself in the process. 

Mell crawls towards the [[Sanguinine]], and proclaims that he will die with pride. He recites what his mother told him when he was young and places his hand on the [[Sanguinine]] hoard. He closes his eyes and taps into its power. He feels his body being torn apart by the power he's carrying as it surges through him. He redirects it into the ground and detonates the entire fortress. As he's dying, he continues reciting what his mother told him, and then, with a misty eyed smile, ionizes and turns to dust. 

With the Needle King and the Culling's headquarters destroyed, Terra is freed. The economy booms, and a new era of peace begins. A statue in memorial of Mell is built, and a new democratic government led by the Rebellion's leader forms. That government now oversees the [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]], who are used to keep the peace.

- Take inspiration from Spider-Man, the 10th Doctor, and Iron Man
- Make the story less derivative of Star Wars by cutting the rebellion and instead making this a personal journey of heroism that Mell goes on. He doesn't need assistance, and it's better if his life sucks. 
- The Needle King and Mell need more of a relationship. He's meant to represent greed and selfishness, and how it does pay but doesn't bring satisfaction. He's always in pursuit of more and can't form any meaningful relationships or hobbies. He's obsessive about his work, and has an absolute dominance over everything, which is why he becomes so fascinated by Mell and has the time and power to pursue him. 
- Part 1: Guilt
	- Mell is a selfish and morally corroded person who commits violent crimes against innocent people due to the world he lives in convincing him it's "kill or be killed," and that "desperate times call for desperate measures." 
	- Someone discovers his [[Ferromancy]], so he shoots them. They did nothing wrong, but knowledge of his abilities puts Mell in danger. 
	- He *is* powerful, but not more powerful than the Needle King. He is a reasonably gifted [[Ferromancy|Ferromancer]], but it could be anyone living the life he is and going on the journey he's about to. Maybe he acquires his powers unnaturally? 
	- His actions grant him wealth, power, and respect. He lives a comfortable life in an uncomfortable world, shielded from any consequence for his villainy. 
	- He is an inherently good natured person who's been ignoring his upbringing and conscience for greed. In no-stakes situations, he chooses to be kind and polite to people. He feels shame and guilt, just not enough to motivate him to change.
	- He witnesses the pain and suffering his lifestyle is causing people, and indirectly puts his one friend in danger. His inclination towards kindness and heroism causes him to save his friend. People find out he's a [[Ferromancy|Ferromancer]] in the process, and he chooses not to kill them.

	- His old life begins to burn down due to him saving his friend. It was an objectively bad decision, as he's now struggling to get money, food, water, and shelter. 
	- He feels guilty for what he's been causing, and understands that if he doesn't save people, no one else will. His empathy is bubbling to the surface in minor ways, causing him to begin to become more altruistic.
	- He fully embraces being a hero and begins to put on some serious displays of advanced [[Ferromancy]].

The Needle King himself personally attempts to recruit him (due to Mell being a high reputation target), but Mell declines out of principle. He begins to try to force him to join by threatening to kill his best friend. 
	- Mell *barely* makes it out of there with his friend, mostly because the Needle King only sent his inquisitors after them instead of attacking personally. 
	- They go on the run. His mental health, relationships, resources, and comfort all take a serious hit. 
	- The inquisitors are hunting him and his friend down now, but he's able to evade and eventually defeat them.
