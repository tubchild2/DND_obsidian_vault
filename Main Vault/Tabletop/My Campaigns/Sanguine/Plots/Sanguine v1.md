##### **PART 1**
**Chapter 1:**
A hero is born in a lower class mining town in the impact zone. His parents are miners. He's a scoundrel and a rebel, and he's out on the streets trying to steal food. He gets caught and tries to run away. In the process, he demonstrates the signs of [[Ferromancy]]. While trying to get revenge, and to get on the good side of the [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]], the person he stole from reports him. 

[[Ferromancy|Ferromantic]] inquisitors arrive the next day in his home, dressed in all black and wearing garbs covered in needles and [[Ferromancy|Sanguinine]]. They speak to his parents, who lie and say that he isn't here. They interrogate them more, and start to hurt them. Much later, he gets home. He doesn't see his parents, but he does see the inquisitors. They approach him and begin to ask him questions. He asks where his parents are, and they deflect the question to attempt to indoctrinate him into their order. He realizes they hurt his parents and so he flips out and gets angry. When they realize he isn't joining them, they begin attacking him. He tries to run, but is very quickly caught, and they ram a spike through his upper chest. He collapses but isn't dead yet. They leave to follow up on their next lead, assuming him unsavable. The local doctor saw the inquisitors in town and is able to find the boy and treat his wounds, albeit poorly. It missed any vital organs, but his left arm is permanently weaker. 


**Chapter 2:** 
After the incident, the hero became far more rebellious internally, but was unfortunately forced into hiding. If his identity was ever discovered, he'd likely be hunted down by inquisitors. He barely uses his abilities, and whenever he does, it's always in very subtle ways. 

He's called to join the rebellion, but he refuses because he thinks they're stupid for going after the [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]]. The rebellion attracts the attention of the [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]] anyways, which puts him in immediate danger and forces him to leave the comforts of his town. Before he can leave, he sees the [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]] about to hurt some of the rebels he really respects. He intervenes and tries to deescalate the situation. That doesn't work, so he's pushed to using his powers to help them escape. 


**Chapter 3:**
Now that he's on the run and back on the [[Ferromancy|Ferromancer]]'s radar, he figures he might as well use his powers. He meets up with his doctor friend, who tests his [[Ferromancy|Ferromantic]] potential. Apparently, he has some of the highest potential for raw power that the doctor has ever seen. If he had started younger like [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]] usually do, he could've rivaled the Needle King by now. 

He reunites with the rebellion and agrees to go with them on a minor mission. While with the rebellion, he figures he'd start getting better with his abilities. He's still going to be an initiate, but he'll be able to progress much faster due to his [[Ferromancy|Ferromantic]] potential. His doctor friend gifts him a sizable node of [[Ferromancy|Sanguinine]], granting him an immediate offensive boost.


**Chapter 4:**
Details the mission and lets the hero use his abilities for the first real time. The mission fails because of his fear of the [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]], and inability and unwillingness to fight back against them. This jeopardizes the entire rebellion, and gets his doctor friend killed. 


**Chapter 5:**
A new all or nothing mission is planned in which they attempt to rob a huge shipment of [[Ferromancy|Sanguinine]] from the [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]]. In mourning, the hero regrets his actions and realizes he needs to let go of fear if he's going to try to make the world a better place. 


**Chapter 6:**
He probably reaches Novice level here. They go on the train heist mission, but Uh Oh! the Needle King saw it coming and sent some inquisitors after them. The hero is personally ambushed by one, and acquires a needle sword for the first time. His weak left-arm complicates sword fighting as well. What ensues is a high-stakes train-borne [[Ferromancy|Ferromancer]] duel. Due to his quick thinking, he's able to find creative ways to use his terrain to his advantage. He's much weaker than the inquisitor, and he not only has to beat him, but he also has to defend the other members of the rebellion. He ultimately gets him crushed under the train, allowing them to complete the heist successfully. 


##### **PART 2**
Probably deals with novice -> knight. He's attempting to free entire mines in this one, all the while fighting off a handful of inquisitors. It ends with him encountering the Needle King and getting his butt whooped. 


##### **PART 3**
Knight -> master. One last big mission that ends with a final rematch with the Needle King.
