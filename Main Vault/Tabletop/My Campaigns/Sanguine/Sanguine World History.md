#### Impact
**3000**
A 10km diameter stone and [[Sanguinine]] meteorite impacted Terra. The impact destroyed the surface of the continent. Everything aboveground was reduced to sand. The surviving vegetation thinly dotted the coasts, and nothing else. The dust in the sky caused debris rain and a brutal winter. 

Those living below ground were the only survivors. All structure and knowledge persisting from pre-impact began to dissolve as more and more people starved or died of thirst. 

**3013**
The survivors began basic aboveground scouting and resettlement. The west coast was a hotspot for early villages and factions. 

Amidst the turmoil, a new generation of children were being born in a post-impact world. The trace amounts of [[Sanguinine]] in the sands began to cause the [[Ferromancy|Ferromantic]] mutation to occur in roughly 1 in 250 newborns. 

**3015**
Scouts discover [[Sanguinine]]. After some experimentation, the survivors realize its extreme usefulness and apparent rarity. Mines started being developed around the north, south, and especially eastern portions of the continent. The impact zone in the east contained an extremely high concentration of [[Sanguinine]].

**3018**
Competition for [[Sanguinine]] prompted a bloody gold rush between the factions. The biggest, baddest, and wealthiest factions began wiping out or conquering whoever else they could to capitalize off the mines. 

**3033**
[[Ferromancy]] was discovered and officially recognized by a handful of factions across Terra. Early [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]] could at best perform basic magnetic manipulation. The true scale of their abilities wouldn't be discovered for a long time. For now, they were hired as soldiers and guards for important places or persons. 


#### The Red Culling
**3047**
A 16 year old Joel Sterling discovers his [[Ferromancy|Ferromantic]] abilities. Unlike other [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]], Joel had an exceptionally high blood-[[Sanguinine]] content, granting him immense [[Ferromancy|Ferromantic]] potential. Realizing what he was capable of, he sought out alliances and ways to amplify his power. His 3 year journey across Terra would result in him recruiting over 80 [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]] to his new secret faction: the Red Culling. Using his advanced knowledge and power, Sterling would develop the techniques for boosting with [[Sanguinine]], strength enhancing, and even lightning redirection. They armed themselves with needle blades, [[Sanguinine]] bracers, and chains. 

**3050**
The Red Culling and its inquisitors began a systematic and brutal genocide of all other [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]] in Terra. Their crusade lasted 6 months, and allowed them to seize control of the entire eastern mining operation and its associated towns and ruins. They began to hoard [[Sanguinine]] in a fortress protected by the eastern mesa. The Culling's influence was felt everywhere, as no one had any option but to give them whatever they demanded. 

**3055**
With his immense wealth and power, Sterling transformed himself into the Needle King. He donned a metal exosuit with extravagant black and red fabric. His piglike mask was adorned with a crown of black spikes. His gauntlets and needle were fitted with the purest nodes of [[Sanguinine]] in Terra. 

**3171 -- Today**
The common man has 3 options for survival. 
1. Work for a faction or in a city, likely mining [[Sanguinine]].
2. Salvage the ruins for treasure.
3. Become an outlaw, and form or join a gang. 

Becoming an outlaw is by far the best (or at least most common) option. It pays so much better and is much easier, if risky and morally bankrupt. Everyone in Terra is desperate and will turn on you given the chance, but they'll still warmly welcome you in and make the best of a bad situation with you. 

The culture has changed in the wake of piracy's rise:
- Desperate times call for desperate measures. 
- There's honor among thieves.
- Take care of your own.
- Never leave a man behind.
- Make the best with what you have.
- Everyone has the right to parley.

The Needle king is 140 years old. [[Ferromancy]] is heavily regulated, and there are 2nd and 3rd generation inquisitors running the Red Culling. 

Cities have been formed, but they're dark, rare, and riddled with crime. 

It's a bad time to be around.
