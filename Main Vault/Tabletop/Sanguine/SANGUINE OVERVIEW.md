### World Aesthetic 
**Aesthetics**
Ruins
- The ruins are inspired by 80s sci-fi cyberpunk. 
- Everything is analog, which means buttons, switches, wires, knobs, and *no* touchscreens! 
- Oscilloscopes and computer terminals are everything. 
- Everything that still works runs on no-GUI Linux-style interfaces. 

Coastal Greenery
- California / New Mexico
- High desert
- Dry, but dotted with greenery

Western Hills
- Utah / Arizona
- Buttes, plateaus, and mesas
- Deep orange everywhere
- Almost completely dry

Eastern Hills
- More unnatural, slanted rock formations
- Spikes, chasms, ravines
- Several gigantic rocks were launched into the sky during the impact and have settled as strange obelisks and piles. 
- Darker black and red in color.

Dunes
- Almost entirely empty except for the odd railway.
- Very tall ripples around the crater. 
- Levels out towards the coasts.

Crater
- Dark black and red stone. 
- Extremely unpredictable and jagged terrain. 
- No one goes into the asteroid's resting place without protection due to risk of [[Sanguinine]] contact. 
- Poor and dangerous and full of suffering

Poor Towns
- Classic spaghetti western towns
- Stone, bricks, and wood

Mines
- Everyone is thoroughly insulated and protected with light and breezy hazmat-like suits.

Cities / Fortresses
- Bay
	- Akin to a pirate stronghold in Sea of Thieves
	- Stone bricks, clear blue water, palm trees
	- One of the few beautiful places
- West and South
	- Dark, gross, and cloudy
	- 1800s western cities
	- Sand and gravel roads
	- Shorter, stouter buildings. Maybe 2-3 stories tall. Wider
- Maybe the northern city in the mountains
	- Almost dark deco in design
	- Extravagant but dark
	- The dark skies made it appear gross and red
	- Crime is rampant
	- Like Gotham but simpler
	- Emphasis on verticality. Everything is tall and thin, creating a spiky skyline. 
- The Red Culling's base
	- A dark deco palace built with layers and layers of metal. 
	- It has an expansive underground military portion dedicated to training [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]]
	- The throne room is built atop the [[Sanguinine]] hoard, meaning the King can tap into it and use it to vaporize people he wants to execute at will given he's in his throne room. 
	- Emphasis on verticality. Everything is tall and thin, creating a spiky skyline. 


**Color**
Often monochromatic; black and one primary color. 
- Red: power, violence, intensity. 
- Light Blue: calmness, stability, trust.
- Dark Blue: cold, sadness, depression. 
- Green: greed, nature, envy. 
- Pink: love, sensitivity, tenderness. 

**Shape**
- Tall and thin
- A lot of pointy things
- Things are needlessly and impractically sharp

**Texture**
- Rough edges
- Dirty and rusty
- Dry




### Goals for this world
Option A:
	Worldbuild this by myself or with friends for the fun of it. 

Option B:
	Write a novel set in this world about a hero with insane [[Ferromancy|Ferromantic]] potential evading the Needle King and eventually freeing the land from his influence. 

Option C:
	Worldbuild this by myself or with other DMs and add as much detail as possible. Run our own campaigns in it, or write one story together that we can run multiple times with each DM and their group (given no player overlap across DMs).
