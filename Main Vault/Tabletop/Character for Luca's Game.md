He's an artisan and delivery boy. He sells mostly medical equipment and chemicals. He's knowledgeable about chemistry. As a merchant, he'd work well as a scout and have decent investigation, insight, and perception. I like to think he makes crude maps everywhere he goes in his journal and takes thorough notes on potential customers. He carries a large backpack and has multiple books and journals. 

He's in Ilese on business. His boss won't reveal any information about who he is or what he's selling, and all the "goods" appear to be useless black shapes. He has to meet sketchy people in sketchy places at sketchy times to deliver them, but he gets paid a ton for it. His boss only communicates email, and has an incredibly distinct manner of speaking: he says the same things over and over and spams non-sequiturs. 

When he was 13, he went exploring in the woods by his house. He couldn't see the lights anymore and it was getting dark, so he got scared and started screaming for help. He met a mysterious man who asked him very personal questions that implied he already knew who he was. He told him how to get out of the woods, and told him he'd be in touch. The next day, he got his first check and email from the employer, who claimed he was informed about Olan through a friend of a friend. Olan was concerned, but the check was more than his dad made so he accepted and started delivering the employer's stuff. After a few months, he finished studying for his medical degree and began helping and selling equipment for people and animals. 

He's a good hearted kid. Only about 15. He's not really a trainer, but he is knowledgeable about Pokémon from a purely medical standpoint.
