#### Premise
Friday, October 13th, 1995

A kid is on a boat after getting separated on a school trip. At 12:00am, an unnatural flash storm damages their boat and forces him to dock at a nearby island town. He's kicked off the boat until the repairs are finished the following day. 

The minute he arrived, the clock tower was consumed by a flash of red fire, deeply corrupting the hourly chimes. The 12:00 is crackling with red energy. Over the coming 24 hours, the red energy grows until the sky begins to turn a bright red. Once the midnight bell tolls, the clouds part and a large red creature descends, instantly vaporizing everything and everyone. Every time this happens, the timeline resets and the players are put back on the boat at 12:00am, preserving all knowledge they've acquired. 

The bell tower is relevant to stopping the resets, that much is evident. Attempting to enter it causes a barrage of flashing lights to descend upon the player, before imparting visions of 8 people in the town. Each of those townspeople are dealing with a serious problem. If they are assisted, their vision no longer appears in the bell tower. To help them, you need to learn the routines of the involved parties, collect items, and most importantly, be kind to them. 

Theme of the game: 
	Love is patient and kind. Love is not jealous. It does not brag, does not get puffed up, does not behave indecently, does not look for its own interests, does not become provoked. It does not keep account of the injury. It does not rejoice over unrighteousness, but rejoices with the truth. It bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things. Love never fails.

#### Execution as a Video Game
The Player
- WASD top-down movement
- Inventory (basic and backpack)
- Cash (defaults to $0)
- Use Item
	- Phone (recall items)
	- Subspace Bubble (skip time)
	- Camera (save / load)
		- Stores a time and then a snapshot of the scheduler's data and is cleared at the end of the day
	- Elevator Pass (used automatically when interacting with elevators)
	- Lockpick (open doors)
	- Credit Card (sets your money to $9999 once unlocked)
	- Mask (equip / unequip)
	- Handbook
		- Opens a menu to allow you to see game stats
		- Can be used to perform insight checks
		- Can be used to check for hidden objects
	- Backpack (used automatically to increase your carrying capacity to INF)

The Environment
- Transition points
- Pop up menus

NPCs
- Can be called by the scheduler to perform actions or switch active dialogue tree
- Dialogue trees

The Scheduler
- Organized by half hour
- Controls and tracks the flow of time
- Calls events when they're supposed to happen
- Tracks all event data, including actionable alterations the player has caused



#### Mechanics
Phone
- Trial: Item Impermanence
- Unlock: Phone
- You can now loop items back around with you, but are still limited to a carrying capacity of 1 item at a time. Additionally, you can't carry heavy objects very far, or hide anything on your person. 

Watch
- Trial: Areas becoming inaccessible, and time moving slowly
- Unlock: Subspace Bubble
- You can now fast forward time and keep your exact position, allowing you to not only speed up your progression, but enter places after they have naturally closed. 

Camera
- Trial: Trial and Error
- Unlock: Camera
- You can now quickly retry 1 event

Unloaded Camera
- Trial: Repeated Trial and Error
- Unlock: Unloaded Camera
- You can save and load an unlimited number of times per cycle. 

Elevator Pass
- Trial: Inaccessible events because they occur too early for you to reach them in time. 
- Unlock: Elevator Pass
- You can now quickly travel around the map

Lockpick
- Trial: Inaccessible areas because the doors are locked throughout the entire day. 
- Unlock: Lockpick
- You can now pick the locks on doors to enter areas you aren't supposed to

Credit Card
- Trial: Inaccessible items because you can't afford to purchase them.
- Unlock: Credit Card
- You can now spend up to $9999 per day.

Mask
- Trial: Inaccessible areas because you consistently get caught. 
- Unlock: Mask
- You can now walk past people without being noticed. It's as if you don't exist. 

Handbook
- Trial: Difficulty tracking quests, routines, notes. You also can't find invisible objects, and there's information buried in NPC's thoughts. 
- Unlock: Handbook
- You can now track game statistics, locate hidden objects, and gain insight into how a character is thinking at a given moment. 

Backpack
- Trial: Some situations require you to carry more than one item at a time, loop multiple items around at once, conceal an item where no one can find it, move something too heavy to carry for an extended period, or move an object out of a room where it won't fit through the doorway. 
- Unlock: Backpack
- Anything you can lift can now be placed into and subsequently removed at will from an infinite extradimensional space that no one else can see or interact with. 


GAME MANIPULATION ITEMS
Handbook must come after Camera, since it provides insight that trivializes many of the puzzles the Camera solved. 
Camera -> Handbook

ITEM MANIPULATION ITEMS
Credit Card must come after Phone, since it trivializes puzzles that may involve valuable items. 
Backpack must come after Credit Card, since it trivializes repeated item acquisition in the first place. 
Phone -> Credit Card -> Backpack

STEALTH ITEMS
Lockpick must come well after subspace bubble, since it trivializes many of its puzzles. 
Mask must come after lockpick, since lockpick introduces stealth and criminal sections. 
Subspace Bubble -> Lockpick -> Mask

TRAVERSAL ITEMS
It will likely be used in combination with the subspace bubble. It doesn't matter which order they're acquired in, since it would be interesting either way. 
Elevator Pass



#### THE QUEST
**EARLY GAME**
Main Quest #1: Shame
- Quest: Save a challenged teenager's life by helping him find to accept that everyone is flawed in their own ways, and that he's no worse than anyone else. He loves people and is very sweet in actuality. Help him build his confidence up to ask a girl out. 
	- He displays the warning signs of depression, has a bad day where he fails romantically, and then takes his life. 
	- You can notice the signs, help him have a good day, and then motivate him to go to a therapist. 
- Theme: Love is... kind
- Unlock: Phone

Main Quest #2: Selflessness
- Quest: You have to put your life in danger to help someone who's involved with a couple of criminals. He's a father now, and he can't do this anymore. 
- Theme: \[Love] does not look for its own interests.
- Unlock: Elevator Pass

Main Quest #3: Jealousy
- Quest: Someone is overcome with intense jealousy over someone else's success, and it's destroying their relationships. You need to help them out of that. 
- Theme: \[Love] is not jealous
- Unlock: Camera

Forgotten Man
- Quest: a mysterious man who possesses 4th wall breaking knowledge gives you a strange set of tasks related to the bell tower. This quest cannot be repeated. 
- Unlock: Watch



**MID GAME**
Main Quest #4: Grief
- Quest: Help the mayor move through all 5 stages of grief and accept the death of his brother. It takes a long time, and you have to put up with him through a lot. 
- Theme: Love is patient
- Unlock: Credit Card

Main Quest #5: Humility
- Quest: Prevent a violent familial attack from a prideful man by talking through a generations long conflict.
- Theme: \[Love] does not brag, does not get puffed up, does not become provoked.
- Unlock: Lockpick

Main Quest #6: Legacy
- Quest: Help a terminally ill man be proud of his legacy by showing how much the townspeople care about him. 
- Theme: \[Love] rejoices with the truth
- Unlock: Handbook



**LATE GAME**
Main Quest #7: Patience
- Quest: There's a person who seems to be an flat antagonist at first who gets humiliated and loses naturally. If you interact with him, you learn his side of the story and why he is the way he is. You need to help him despite him being rude, selfish, and unthankful. Multiple times throughout this process, you have options to indulge and have a good time, which is always the wrong decision in the end. 
- Theme: \[Love] does not rejoice over unrighteousness
- Unlock: Backpack

Main Quest #8: Determination
- Quest: Save a relationship that seems destined to fail. It requires extreme persistence and determination to work through it all, but ends with a bittersweet moment of love between the couple. 
- Theme: \[Love] bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things. Love never fails. 
- Unlock: Mask


**END GAME**
Main Quest #9: Finale
- Quest: Enter the clock tower and destroy the source. Requires mastery of every item, and understanding of the game's themes. 

Daisy Bell
- Quest: using a combination of every item you can follow the mysterious music you've been hearing all game. It's Daisy Bell, and it leads you into a hidden grotto with a sunken military gunship. It contains a "computer" you can speak with. It gives you some bad advice that's the antithesis of the game's themes and opens the path to "Escape" by giving you the unloaded camera. 
- Unlock: Unloaded Camera

Escape
- Quest: using help from the "computer," you're able to repair the boat and make it off the island. The forgotten man attempts to stop you. If you defeat him, you make it off the island and get the bad ending.
