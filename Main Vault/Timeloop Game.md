#### Premise
Friday, October 13th, 1995

A group of 1-4 kids are put on a boat after getting separated on a school trip. At 12:00am, an unnatural flash storm damages their boat and forces them to dock at a nearby island town. They're kicked off the boat until the repairs are finished the following day. 

The minute they arrived, the clock tower was consumed by a flash of red fire, deeply corrupting the hourly chimes. The 12:00 is crackling with red energy. Over the coming 24 hours, the red energy grows until the sky begins to turn a bright red. Once the midnight bell tolls, the clouds part and a large red creature descends, instantly vaporizing everything and everyone. Even if they repair the boat and try to flee, they're destroyed. 

Every time this happens, the timeline resets and the players are put back on the boat at 12:00am, preserving all items and knowledge they've acquired. 

The bell tower is relevant to stopping the resets, that much is evident. Attempting to enter it causes a barrage of flashing lights to descend upon the players, before imparting visions of 8 people in the town. 

Each of those townspeople are dealing with a serious problem. If they are assisted, their vision no longer appears in the bell tower. To help them, you need to learn the routines of the involved parties, collect items, and most importantly, learn about them. 

The core theme of the game is to be kind. Each of the 8 characters reflects or challenges that theme in some way, and has a unique interpersonal drama that you need to help with.

#### Mechanics
Character customization doesn't use DND. All kids with rudimentary classes and backgrounds.

Persistent Items
- Delivery Beacon. Lets you call William with Uber Eats. He shows up at 12:00am at the beginning of each cycle to deliver every important item you've collected in previous cycles. This item persists across cycle resets. 
- Subspace Bubble. Lets you fast forward to 6:00am (dawn), 12:00pm (noon), 6:00pm (night), or to the next cycle at 12:00am (midnight). This item persists across cycle resets.

Regular Items
- Temporal Camera. Lets you capture and reload a single moment in time. Functions like a quick save with a single slot. Lets you save scum through certain issues with no clear solution. Can be used to quickly undo mistakes. You can only save once per cycle, but you can reload an infinite number of times. The camera empties any saves stored in it at the end of the cycle. 
- Elevator Pass. Lets you access a nonsensical elevator that appears throughout the map. Once you acquire the pass, you can use the elevator to travel to any other elevator location you've previously visited in this cycle. If you have the delivery beacon, than you can travel to any elevator location you've ever visited. 
- Key to the City. Lets you open any door on the island. Unfortunately, just being able to enter a room doesn't mean the people inside want you there. Doesn't work on the elevator. 
- Credit Card. Lets you spend up to $9999 per day.
- Rock Mask. Makes everyone and everything ignore you as if you didn't exist. 

Work In Progress
- Badge of Honor. Earns you the trust of certain people.
- Compact Rebreather. Lets you dive underwater. 
- Sneakers of Swiftness. Grants you enhanced speed. 
- Strongman's Bracer. Grants you enhanced strength.
- Lens of Truth. Lets you see things no one else can.
- Large Ruby. A priceless gemstone that can be traded for an item of high value. 



#### Missions
Main Quest \#1: Legacy
- Help a terminally ill shopkeeper to rest peacefully before he passes tonight
- Ensure he leaves behind a positive legacy

Main Quest \#2: Humility
- Save a depressed mentally challenged teenager's life
- Help them to value and accept themselves

Main Quest \#3: Forgiveness
- Prevent a man from making a violent mistake
- Reunite him with his family that's been fighting for years

Main Quest \#4: Grief
- Calm someone grieving the death of their sibling
- Help them to move on and restart their life

Main Quest \#5: Confidence
- Encounter a social reject
- Help build his confidence so that he can ask a girl out

Main Quest \#6: Impartiality
- Meet a rude and selfish man who's going to make a huge mistake
- Save him regardless of how much he doesn't want you to

Main Quest \#7: Selflessness
- Meet a man who's indebted to a criminal organization
- Sacrifice everything you can to pay off his debt

Main Quest \#8: Determination
- Meet a little girl who dies in the wilderness
- Save her over and over from 6+ threats until she survives the day



#### Side Missions
The Mystery Man
- Meet a mysterious man who seems to possess knowledge beyond the cycles. 
- He gives you a set of strange tasks that are related to the bell tower that graze the fourth wall.
- This quest cannot be repeated, as the man vanishes upon completion. 

Escape!
- You can attempt to gather $1000 worth of materials and repair the boat.
- If you do, you embark early and make it off the island. 
- You survive a couple extra seconds and watch the wave of destruction approach from the island. 

Heist
- You have a terrible map of a structure going into it, meaning you're going to need to use your time manipulation abilities and improvisation to complete the heist. 

Daisy Bell
- You can hear Daisy Bell after some events that appear to be staged by something
- If you follow the music, you're led to cave dive in the lake into a hidden grotto
- The grotto contains a sunken military gunship that's at least a century old. There's no record of what it is or why it's there.
- It contains a sentient "computer." Some kind of being is speaking through the terminal. 
- You can ask it bigger questions about the nature of the island, but it has ulterior motives
