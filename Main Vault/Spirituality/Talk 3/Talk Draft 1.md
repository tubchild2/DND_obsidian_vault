**Introduction**
It feels like politics is more important now than ever before. There's constantly just so much happening, and often times it seems like everyone is talking about what's going on in the news: asking you for your opinions. So, it's often surprising to people that we as Jehovah's Witnesses remain politically neutral. Maybe you've been a witness for years and have been asked about it before, or maybe you haven't. Regardless, it's important to understand why we remain neutral, not just so you can respond to people to have questions, but so you can understand it well for yourself. 

We'll be answering a few questions in this talk
- Why we remain neutral
- What that means about our view of governments


**Why We Remain Politically Neutral**
Jehovah's Witnesses are neutral in imitation of Bible teachings. 

Well, what does neutrality mean? That means that we don't lobby, vote for political parties or candidates, run for government office, or participate in any action to change governments. 

Neutrality doesn't mean we don't care about people, or that we hate everyone equally. No, we actually do this in imitation of one of the most loving men to ever live: Jesus. Jesus refused to accept political office, and taught his disciples to be "no part of this world." He made it clear they shouldn't take sides in political matters. We know this isn't a modern innovation too, because the apostles and other first-century Christians took an identical stand toward governmental authority. Multiple secular sources verify this, including a 863-page history textbook called On the Road to Civilization. It's quoted as saying that the early Christians, "would not hold political office."

Now another reason we remain neutral is because we are loyal to God's Kingdom. We act as representatives of God's Kingdom, so we don't get involved in political affairs of any countries. If we were, it'd be sort of hypocritical since we'd be preaching about a coming government that could solve men's problems, while also attempting to solve man's problems ourselves. Yes, by remaining neutral, we can speak freely to everyone from everywhere without fear of division. This grants us our international brotherhood its unique unity. 



**Our Attitude Towards Governments**
Now, what does that mean about our view of governments? 

We respect the authority of the governments under which we live, as the Bible teaches at Romans 13:1. We obey laws, pay taxes, and cooperate with the efforts of the government. We follow the Bibile's counsel to pray for all those who are in positions of authority, especially in regards to decisions that could affect freedom of worship. 

We also respect the rights of others to make their own decisions. We don't disrupt elections or protest to pressure others to conform to our views. That wouldn't be following the Bible's counsel. 

After all this, there are some that may see us sharing our beliefs and wonder if we're going to attempt to do anything beyond that. To put it plainly, we are not a threat to national security. We are peace-loving citizens who remain law abiding citizens no mater the circumstances. Today, the beliefs and practices of Jehovah’s Witnesses do not, as a 2001 report from the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine, “undermine the security and integrity of any state.”


**Conclusion**
So, at the beginning we asked what you'd do if you were asked about neutrality. Something something. We remain politically neutral in imitation of Bible teachings, and in imitation of Jesus. That doesn't mean we don't respect governments, quite the contrary. Jehovah's Witnesses are law-abiding, tax-paying, and cooperative citizens that remain peaceful no matter the circumstances. Something something, that's what you can tell people. I'm done.
