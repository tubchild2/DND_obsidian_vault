**Goblin Boss**
- AC 17
- Speed 30
- STR 10
- INT 10
- DEX 15
- WIS 8
- CON 10
- CHA 10
- Stealth +6
Actions
- Multiattack: two attacks using scimitar or shortbow in any combination
- Scimitar: +4, reach 5ft, 1d6+5 slashing damage (+1d4 if attack has advantage)
- Shortbow: +4, reach 80ft, 1d6+2 piercing damage (+1d4 if attack had advantage)
Bonus Actions
- Nimble Escape: Disengage or Hide
Reaction
- Redirect Attack:
	- Trigger: creature the goblin sees makes an attack roll against it
	- Response: the goblin chooses an ally within 5ft and swaps places, making the ally the target instead
