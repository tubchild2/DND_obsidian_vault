### Classes
###### Brute
Features
- Very high health
- High damage
- Rage
Drawbacks
- Low speed
Subclasses
- More attacks
- Greater health and healing
- Better rage



###### Bard
Features
- Utility and buffing abilities
- Good charisma
- Skill junkie
Drawbacks
- Low health
- Few great skills
Subclasses
- Better skills and charisma
- More damage and attacks
- Greater buffing and utility skills



###### Medic
Features
- Excellent healing
Drawbacks
- Average health
- Average damage
Subclasses
- Life steal
- Even greater healing
- More damage and attacks



###### Fighter
Features
- High damage
- Weapon variety
- Equipment junkie
Drawbacks
- Not really *good* at one specific thing
Subclasses
- More combat options
- Better crits and more damage
- More health, defense, and damage reduction
- Battle medic



###### Monk
Features
- Fights without weapons
- Agility and acrobatics
Drawbacks
- Lower strength
- Mid health
Subclasses
- Stealth abilities
- Double down on damage
- Medical expertise



###### Thief
Features
- Stealth
- Sleight of hand
- Sneak attacks
Drawbacks
- Very low health
- Useless once exposed
Subclasses
- Better sneak attacks
- Better stealth and sleight of hand
- Better charisma and deception



###### Engineer
Features
- Create / modify items / weapons
- Create / modify sentries / drones
- Create / modify traps / explosives
- Creative expression with a modifier system
Drawbacks
- Low health
- Resource intensive
- Vulnerable if unprepared
Subclasses
- Blacksmith (items and weapons)
- Defender (sentries, drones, and shields)
- Saboteur (traps and explosives)



###### Alchemist
Features
- Create / modify potions / food
- Create / modify poisons / acids
- Create / modify explosives
- Create / modify chemical elements
- Creative expression with a modifier system
Drawbacks
- Low health
- Resource intensive
- Vulnerable if unprepared
Subclasses
- A (potions and food)
- B (poisons, acids, and explosives)
- C (chemical reactions)



###### Telekinetic
Features
- Move things with your mind
- Functionally unstoppable at first
Drawbacks
- Very low health
- Exhausts very quickly
Subclasses
- Psion (complexity)
- Rampart (force)
- Specter (endurance)



###### Hero
Features
- Signature weapon bound to you that grows with you in power
Drawbacks
- Losing the weapon is a huge problem
Subclasses
- Oathbound (sentient weapon)
- Gearbound (customizable weapon)
- Warbound (multiple signature weapons)



###### Scoundrel
Features
- Extremely excellent charisma skills
- Luck manipulation
- Double or nothing
- Precision and trick shots
Drawbacks
- You can gamble and lose hard
Subclasses
- Acrobat (sneaking and movement)
- Lucky (safer luck manipulation)
- Gambler (bigger risks, bigger rewards)



###### Civilian
Features
- You are a normal person
Drawbacks
- None
Subclasses
- None



###### Shifter
Features
- Localized spacetime alterations
- Portals and foresight
- Sensing what's around them
Drawbacks
- Extremely low health
- Consequences to teleporting other people
Subclasses
- Portals and banishment
- Time shifting and foresight
- Attacks and physics breaking



###### Handler
Features
- Animal companions that grow with you and inherit your abilities
- You can control them during your turns
- Like a more capable version of Engineer's sentries
- Meant for multiclassing
Drawbacks
- Difficult to acquire new animal companions if they die
- You're vulnerable when you're alone
Subclasses
- Buff one really good animal companion
- Collect more animal companions 
- Make yourself more capable without any animals



###### Architect
Features
- Summon light constructs
- Limited by your imagination
- They get more complex, durable, and sizeable with time
Drawbacks
- Concentration based
- Limited charges per day
Subclasses
- Complexity
- Durability
- Scale



###### Altered
Features
- Has multiple personalities stored within their mind. Each personality has its own stats. 
- You the player are the dominant alter and are usually in control. 
- You can have alters front and access their full statblocks and memories. 
- You can communicate with any of your alters at will. 
Drawbacks
- Siphoned abilities are inherently weaker
- People can resist being stored, so you need to get them to the verge of death or convince them.
- You can only front one alter at a time
- You can only store a couple of alters at a time
- While an alter is fronting, they can attempt to break free, force control, or attack you. 
- Storing people has moral and legal consequences
- The bodies of stores alters become comatose and lose their powers. 
- Alters that exit or are freed instantly return to their body and regain their independence and power. 
Subclasses
- More alters with better power siphoning but worse control over them
- Store a cosmic alter that grants you powers but requires constant upkeep
- Better control over your alters and the ability to front multiple at a time



###### Illusionist
Features
- Telepathy
- Suggestion
- Hallucinations
- Delusions
- High perception
Drawbacks
- Mental powers can fail if someone has good wisdom
- Scope, complexity, and believability of illusions starts small, but gets huge with time
Subclasses
- Bigger and better illusions
- Better mind control
- Better psychic attacks



###### Protomancer
Features
- Creation / manipulation of electricity
- Creation / manipulation of magnetic fields
- Faster reflexes and running speed
- Metal based movement tech
Drawbacks
- Doesn't tire out nearly as fast as the telekinetic, but still does
- Low health
Subclasses
- Stronger and more lightning
- Better manipulation of metal
- Faster attacks and far greater movement



###### Husk
Features
- Constantly regenerating health
- Monstrous rage that alters physical characteristics
- Very fast and agile
Drawbacks
- Built to tank a ton of damage and lose limbs
- Built to attack fast and pursue people like a monster
- Low max health, so vulnerable to burst damage
- Doesn't deal that much damage but attacks fast
Subclasses
- Better, more monstrous rage
- Better health regeneration and more max health
- On demand mutations
