#### Overview
A comic series about why you should be a good person even in a world where cruelty is rewarded. 

Each issue after the origin issue will follow Mell as he navigates a personal conflict, goes up against a villain in Terra, and engages in [[Sanguinine]] and [[Ferromancy]] fueled western-inspired action. 

Who is Mell
- Mell was raised well, but abandoned his morals due to a series of bad influences, namely his brother. After his mother died, they adopted a kill or be killed, desperate times type philosophy. They wanted to protect their own at all costs, so they joined a gang of outlaws and became thieves. Now that he's trying to life a responsible life, he's wracked with guilt. Mell doesn't like himself first and foremost. He feels that he's a bad person, and every mistake he makes feels monumental. He has supreme anxiety, depressive tendencies, and is prone to making impulsive mistakes.  
	- Forgiveness of oneself, and acceptance of mistakes and flaws
	- The False Prophet. Mell was inspired by the hero, and wants to be like him. The hero appears completely perfect and is obsessed with vanity and showmanship. The perfection is boring and impossible. The hero is really a shell of a man and cares so deeply about his image that he'll do horrid things behind the scenes to remain popular. He's narcissistic, and he bullies and hurts Mell. They do everything for appearances and don't care at all about the values they're preaching.
	- An old friend. Someone who is convinced Mell is a bad person and who wants to make him feel like it. He was deeply hurt by Mell unintentionally, and holds grudges. Mell keeps trying to make it up to him, but it keeps getting worse. Mell is frustrated with him and doesn't like him really at first. However, as he gets to know him better, he sees the beauty in the friend's imperfections, and learns to appreciate the flaws about himself more too. He's got wabi-sabi. 
- He desires not just to be a good person, but to feel like a good person. He becomes a hero out of principle; he knows it's the right thing to do, and he knows he's capable of doing it. It makes him feel good, and he feels responsible if he doesn't prevent something bad from happening. 
	- What it means to be a good person, and what his responsibilities are
	- Pinkerton Type Gang. Mell's former gang that turned to targeting bad people. They appear to be heroes, but they're only doing it to feel good about themselves without really doing anything good. They're still killing and looting from rivals, it's just rivals that *everyone* hates instead of rivals just they hate. To do good, you need to do good fully; you can't compromise. Mell wants to join them, but he refuses.
	- Jesse Delaney. Mell's brother who wants to prove to Mell that it isn't his responsibility to be a hero, and that it's just going to ruin his life. He debates Mell on what it really means to be a "good" person, especially in the world they live in. After what happened to their mother, Jesse wants to protect his own through whatever means necessary, which is what she did, and what they failed to do for her. 
- Mell loves people so much, and is very suggestible, so one of the hardest things about his life is the loneliness. He wants to be close to people, but he can't. He's pushed his brother away, his father is wants to keep him safe, he can't maintain his friendships, and he can't keep a girlfriend.
	- Balancing his vigilantism and personal life, and sacrificing his dreams he for the greater good.
	- Mell's complete lack of self-care has caused him to become depressed. He feels persistent sadness, loss of interest in hobbies, fatigue, he doesn't take care of himself, he doesn't sleep, his appetite changes, and he thinks about dying. He is resisting any indulgence because he wants to go out and help people. He needs to learn to take care of himself, and be ok with doing that. 
	- A former bounty hunter and leader of the police force. They were doing a lot of good, but they weren't happy because they wanted a family. So, they quit. They abandoned all of their responsibilities to live a happy life. Was that wrong?
	- A girlfriend. Wants to be with Mell, and the entire relationship would go perfectly if left alone. Unfortunately, he can't pursue her since he can't endanger her and he doesn't have the time for it. He has to break her heart, and he can't explain why. He has to ignore her influence. 
- Mell's believes that in this world, being a bad person is rewarded, and that it's impossible to make a difference. Fixing things permanently on a grand scale is objectively impossible for humans to do. Because of this, he wants to quit. It's the reminders of the people that he's helped that keep him determined. 
	- Finding determination and hope despite all odds
	- The Red Culling / The Needle King. An oppressive, overpowered, and relentless antagonistic force. He can't ever really defeat them, and they represent a deeply entrenched evil in the world. They're constantly hunting him, and he can't do anything but put up with them. 
	- The Rebellion. They want so desperately to undo the Red Culling's control, but they can't. Mell's optimism moves him to try and fight back, but they're complete and utter defeat gives him a pessimistic viewpoint. He has to stop the Rebellion from getting themselves killed, and to redirect to something better. 
- Mell's living situation is terrible. He has very little money, meaning he has poor food, water, and shelter. He wants to steal and be comfortable again. He feels entitled to some wealth because of the good he's put out in the world, causing his occasional bursts of arrogance. Seeing people who are doing all the wrong things and being rewarded for it drive him insane. 
	- Quelling envy, selfishness, and arrogance with humility
	- A Green Goblin. Hates Mell specifically and fundamentally disagrees with his worldview. Absolutely believes that it's kill or be killed, and just wants to make Mell see that and quit because he's a lunatic. He's extremely successful because he takes what he thinks he deserves, and Mell will always be jealous of that fact. He's rich and powerful for what?
	- Mell's father is a sweet and funny old man with a golden set of morals. He's dying from an undisclosed illness they can't treat due to the lack of medical sciences. Much of his money is going to helping him, which makes things much harder and makes him want to steal even more. 
- The people Mell has to deal with are the worst of the worst. Often times, they come back at him harder than he goes after them. He learns very quickly that he needs to remain anonymous since not only will the Red Culling hunt him down, but the several enemies he's making will go after his friends and family. He wants revenge, and he wants to kill people who keep escaping justice. 
	- Learning self-control, and restraint for something bigger than oneself. 
	- An Arbiter / Punisher Type. Someone who kills killers and bad people to solve the problems permanently. Mell has to deal with the fact that he has to allow bad things to continue happening sometimes because it's not his place to decide who lives and who dies. The Punisher / Arbiter wants to kill Inquisitor Primus and has been hunting him down for months. 
	- Inquisitor Primus. A title passed down through 3 generations from the first inquisitor. He was responsible for killing his mother when she tried to hide him and Jesse for being [[Ferromancy|Ferromancers]]. He is a wrathful and vindictive person who hates and oppresses everyone around him. He kills hundreds, and Mell wants to kill him more than anything. He knows more about that night than Mell or Jesse do, and Mell always wanted to know exactly why she had to die.

### Small Stories
**The Church**
Churches are symbols of god and of morality. A previously good character who's succumbed to evil returns to his church during a climactic battle with the hero and protagonist. A neat juxtaposition. 

**Gold Rush**
A new cavern is discovered, and a bloody gold rush to get the [[Sanguinine]] begins. The territory is split between rival factions. The fighting escalates when outlaw gangs get involved. Someone invokes the right to parley, causing them to go into enemy territory to speak with the captain. Much of the fighting takes place in the mines, which ultimately leads to a catastrophic mishandling of [[Sanguinine]]. 

**Meeting the Culling**
A child with [[Ferromancy|Ferromantic]] potential pisses off the Culling, and has an extremely tense conversation with them as the fate of his parents is unclear. He has to escape them, and his lack of power makes it incredibly tense.

**The Defector**
A previous member of the Red Culling faked his death to get away from them when the guilt wore too heavy on his soul. 

**Campfire Stories**
Inquisitors are presented as inhuman to those who haven't seen them. The Needle King himself is treated like the Devil; some doubt he's even real, but everyone is afraid of him. They speak of monsters, myths, and legends; tall tales of heroes and demons. 

**One Down**
A group attempt to kill an Inquisitor that's been harassing the members of their village. They use [[Sanguinine]] constructs and traps, but it still almost fails. 

**Salvagers**
Salvagers enter into a fresh set of underground ruins and make their way deep beneath the surface. They study pre-impact society, how it relates to [[Ferromancy]], and the impact itself. They reactivate some tech from the ruins and get to use the GUI. On the train ride home, they're attacked by pirates.
