### Task Board Guide
Switched to primarily using task board. I don't check the digital one as often as I should, which means I forget to do a lot of things. This will help me to visualize the things I want to do and actually work on them. Also, it looks really good in my room to have a color coordinated corkboard by my calendar and whiteboard. Hopefully, I check this one more often and get those smaller tasks done. 

Tasks that break down day-by-day, things that aren't relevant now but will be in the future, and tasks that are too complicated to fit on the notes will remain here.
