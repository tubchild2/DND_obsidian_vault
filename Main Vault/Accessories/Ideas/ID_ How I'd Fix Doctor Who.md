###### What I Appreciate About the Show
**The Doctor**
- An extremely complex character written over 60+ years
- In my eyes, he's closest to Spider-Man
	- He's burdened with an immense power being functionally the oldest, smartest, and most powerful being in the universe
	- He's also burdened with an immense responsibility to save people where he can, and is cursed with incessant loneliness as he has to let all his friends go or watch them die as he outlives them. He has to make sacrifices for everyone around him because it's simply right. 
- His morality is incredible. He isn't perfect by any means, but I genuinely believe he is one of the highest standards of morality ever portrayed in fiction, rivalling and potentially exceeding Superman. 
- His abilities are really fun. Regeneration is such a cool concept. 

**The Mysteries**
- I loved series 1 with Christopher Eccleston because of the overarching Bad Wolf plot.
- It was mysterious, kind of creepy, and actually really cool to see it revealed at the end. 
- When the show commits to serialization, it knocks it out of the park. 

**The Creativity**
- The premise is an engine for the writers to do whatever they want. It's unlimited time and space travel.

**The Horror**
- The show occasionally attempts to be actually scary and it's awesome when it does.
- The Weeping Angels, that one invisible embodiment of fear, Midnight, freaking Satan, I could go on. 


###### What Went Wrong
**The Timeless Child**
- By giving the Doctor infinite regenerations, it makes them feel completely weightless
- This is worsened by their insistence to bring back previous characters and doctors
- It's even worse since their personalities are becoming more and more flat and non-distinct
- The Master destroys Gallifrey again? The whole 50th special was dedicated to getting it back!

**The Writing**
- They demonstrate a complete lack of understanding of the Doctor's character
- Too much focus on politics, too little focus on characters and plot
- The show is predictable
- The show lacks stakes
- The show isn't scary

**Money**
- Corporate greed has done its fair share of damage to Doctor Who. 
- I don't doubt corporate intervention from Disney is part of the reason for the insertion of politics into the show. 
- There's too much fan service, which is making the show worse. 


###### My Idea
Focus back in on the Doctor as a person. Do what Spider-Man does; break him emotionally. This Doctor is much more hotheaded and impulsive than the others. He's akin to the 10th. 

ACT 1
The beginning of the series will follow the standard Doctor Who formula. He'll meet a companion, explain the premise of the show, and go on a couple adventures. I want to make them as high concept and fun as possible. There should be at least one scary episode. 

There are more surviving Time Lords besides the Doctor and the Master. They're causing problems that are going to begin popping up throughout the series. At this point, the Doctor is going to encounter them for the first time. They are much more threatening than the Master since there's multiple of them and they have a wider skill set. 

During the climax of Act 1, their plan is going to go into full swing and the Doctor is going to lose for the first time in a long time. His companion will die, and the Doctor will be beaten very badly. 



ACT 2
The other Time Lords are going to be causing a microscopic time-war only he can fight, but he's outnumbered and outgunned. 

Desperate and angry, the Doctor will return to a Time Lord victorious mindset. He'll do a bunch of irresponsible stuff with the timeline, and it'll be worsened by the fact that (now, as the Timeless Child), he can't really die. He'll be exploiting this and regenerating all the time, unaware of any potential consequences this could have since he's unique. 

At the climax of Act 2, he'll end up making things much worse due to his irresponsibility, and he'll learn that he's starting to deteriorate from the constant overuse of regeneration. He'll also have messed with the timeline too greatly. 



ACT 3
He'll learn some lesson about responsibility and kindness and reconnect with people. He'll make a big sacrifice and give up his endless regenerations to save the universe.
