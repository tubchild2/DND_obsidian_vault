A prank reality TV show in which the other cast members are actors. The contestant slowly reveals that the show has a very clearly awful dark side. 

The Contestants
- Contestant 1: a real person who's unaffiliated with anything
- Contestant 2: a full-on satanist who's doing disgusting things
- Contestant 3: a cocaine addict who's smuggling drugs onto the set
- Contestant 4: an adulterer who's cheating on their wife with another contestant
- Contestant 5: a rich girl who's bribing and flirting with judges and contestants to get an unfair advantage.
- Contestant 6: a physically violent and deeply insecure man who's huge and intimidating
- Contestant 7: a narcissistic, nerdy, and creepy man who serves primarily to make people uncomfortable.
- Contestant 8: a nice person who's there to befriend contestant 1 only to betray him and get eliminated
- Contestant 9: a douchebag who harasses women, lies, insults people, and steals everyone's stuff

Plot
- The other contestants and the producers quickly ostracize him. He's sent to elimination every round for increasingly stupid reasons. They'll steal contestant 1's immunity idols.  
- The producers begin having arguments with each other. The arguments scale in severity until challenges and living conditions begin being impacted. The host gets increasingly disheveled and the challenges are getting lower and lower budget. They begin running out of food and amenities. 
- Show him strange Truman Show esque rooms hidden in the house, and then completely remove them whenever he returns and gaslight him into thinking it was never there. Keep screwing with him by messing with his stuff. 
- When he eventually wins, they reveal it was a prank and give him $200k. 


### Execution Variants
Could also work as a tabletop game where I talk to the other players ahead of time and get them to play along. Then, I get one player who isn't aware of what's going on to play as contestant 1. I offer a cash prize ahead of time.
