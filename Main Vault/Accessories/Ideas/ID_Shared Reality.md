**DA RULES**
"In the beginning there was the dream. From the dream sprang thought. From thought sprang duality. From duality sprang good and evil. From good and evil sprang life and death. From these things sprang the observers. The observers had no thought; they were simple ideological processors. They reacted to stimuli and produced new concepts. They could uniquely live, multiply, and die. As they grew in number, physical law began to take place. The observers agreed upon space, time, matter, creation, destruction, and all else. The universe shaped itself around their beliefs and sprang into existence. As conscious thought inflated, so too did the intellects of those who were conscious. Primeval forces such as good and evil swelled in power as more observers contemplated them, allowing them to attain the will and power to spread their ideas throughout the universe. So, they dispersed. The observers became man, and man decided to forget. So, the beginning truly is unclear, as it was agreed."
1. The universe is a shared dream. The dream >> force of thought >> force of duality >> forces of good and evil >> forces of life and death >> the observers. Observers agreed upon reality based on the other forces. They became mankind. Primeval forces dispersed and swelled as they became more foundational to existence. 
2. Primeval or ancient ideas are almost impossible to overwrite and will enforce themselves back onto reality.
3. Newer ideas can be created and destroyed, because the anchors maintaining their belief can be altered or destroyed. 
4. Existence is built upon the dynamics of these ideas spawning from observers. Ideas in the cosmos wax and wane and keep one another in balance. 
5. A belief becomes universal the instant it is created, but it usually has small, initially local effects. A magic system that works in one place will work everywhere, unless the practitioners and objects anchoring it to reality are destroyed and it's forgotten. 
6. The more people believe something more intensely, the more true it is.
7. Multiple contradictory systems and understandings functionally coexist and are equally true. Attempts to disprove or analyze contradictory systems births new ideas for both and maintains their coexistence. 
8. Phenomena form the basis for one another's acceptance, meaning the world gets stranger over time. The universe won't trend towards realism because deviations are unpredictable and spiral quickly. 
9. The nature of the universe *is* illogical, and understanding it enough to exploit it requires insanity. 


**DA MAGIC**
"Everything in our world is made up of something called consensus. Reality only exists how it does because we all at some point agreed that this is what reality should be like. Once you understand that, it's possible to step outside the natural bounds and do things you aren't supposed to, because you understand that you can. The issue is that by doing that, and by gaining that understanding and power, you have to let go of every fundamental principle of existence you have, and pretty much go off what fuels your beliefs. So yes, you can do anything, but it makes you crazy."
1. You can do anything if you believe you can do it. 
2. Gaining the intense beliefs required to do anything, though, requires breaking your understanding of reality. 
3. People who subscribe to this form of magic inevitably go insane. 
4. The more rules a form of magic has, and the more agreed upon the practice is, the less insane it makes you. 


**DA RANKING**
1. The Sleeper. Whatever unknowable being is perpetuating the dream that gives existence form and continuity. 
2. Primordials. Abstract beings representing the collective belief and acknowledgement of primeval natural constants, such as good and evil, colors, space, and time. They can be people, monsters, animals, places, objects, etc. They may not even know they exist. 
3. Celestials. Beings created by Primordials to enforce their will and perpetuate their concepts. Powerful enough to destroy entire realms. 
4. Deities. Manifest entities produced and empowered by the collective beliefs of a number of people in a given religion. They often oversee or come bundled with realms. 
5. Manifest Entities. Creatures or people created or empowered by the collective beliefs of a number of people inside of the realm they spawned in. 
6. Aberrational Observers. Observers who have violated their own understanding of reality to give themselves a fluid perception of belief. They can will things into existence, but are insane. 
7. Observers. Entities that create, perpetuate, and destroy the concepts that give the universe form. They can come from anywhere and go on to do anything if they believe they can. The foundation of everything else. 


**DA WORLD**
The way I see it, it's a lot like Adventure Time. All manner of flora and fauna and science and magic and religion can all coexist and are equally true. Each region has its own set of phenomena and unique traits. There are tons and tons of powerful artifacts, monsters, aliens, dimensions, and histories to find. If you can imagine it, it probably exists. 

History is what is accepted as having happened. The minor details might be different or have been forgotten, but something else always fills in those blanks. Almost everything is documented and was preserved.
