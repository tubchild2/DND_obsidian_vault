When I was in 4th grade, I made a Minecraft world called Aquapolis. It was a really interesting creative world where everything was flooded. I built underground glass biospheres, ships powered by engines you needed to keep operational, sailboats, and floating diners with farms on top of them. It was a really cool concept for a world, and it got me thinking; it would be so cool to make a dystopia that's flooded. It'd take multiple billion cubic kilometers of water to do it, but let's suppose that did happen. 

**Climate**
- Sea level rose by a third of a mile in 6 hours due to a cosmic flood. 
- It'd be 6-10 degrees C hotter, more sulfurous, and more humid
- Nothing is stopping the wind anymore, forming more consistent bands of wind across the planet. 
- It'd be cloudier and would storm more. Hurricane season would be insane. 
- The coasts would be nearly perpetually stormy, and would feature steep cliffs.
- Sealed and shallow drowned cities would produce toxic gas filled dead-zones for decades.
- Current swept and accessible drowned cities would produce biological explosions

**Plant Life**
- Almost completely drowns, releasing an insane amount of methane and Co2
- High-altitude crops flourish, most others are drowned

**Terrestrial Life**
- Animals that didn't live in high-altitudes are almost completely destroyed

**Marine Life**
- Massive spike in nutrients causes marine life to boom amidst the ruins of civilization
- Booming phytoplankton would compensate for the lack of plant produced oxygen

**Humanity**
- All humans living below new sea level are destroyed
- All infrastructure, societal structure, and communication is destroyed
- You'd need a submarine to reach any remnants of society
- They would likely have darker skin, and predominately east Asian / African features.
- Sailing cultures
	- Trade, transit, and fishing
- Scientific cultures
	- Documentation and preservation
- Highland cultures
	- Farming, housing, and order
- Piracy cultures
	- Scavenge and steal


### Extra Stuff
**The Weather Station**
- Pre-flood, Spain had an enormous self-sustaining fusion plant
- During the flood, it got destroyed and stuck on
- It's constantly producing an artificial EM, temperature, and pressure anomaly in the upper atmosphere
- Storms are deflected towards it and away from the islands. 
- It is the single most dangerous place in the entire world. 
- Some say it's the reason the cosmic flood happened in the first place
	- Were humans playing god?

**Barges**
- Large flat docks floating in the water that are suspended by protected buoyancy devices
- Ships dock there to get food, trade, swap passengers, etc.
- Piracy and crime are big one some of the sketchier barges

**Domes**
- Underwater interconnected reinforced glass pods built in shallow regions of the floodwaters. 
- Typically constructed near flooded cities to recover, document, and preserve history and technology. 
- Within diving depth for a trained diver. Usually accessed via submersibles.
